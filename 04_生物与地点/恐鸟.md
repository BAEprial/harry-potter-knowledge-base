# 恐鸟

> 英文名：Diricawl

## 条目内容（英文原文）

- **简介**：The Diricawl (known to Muggles as the Dodo) was a plump, fluffy-feathered and flightless magical bird, native to the island of Mauritius in the Indian Ocean. It was capable of disappearing and reappearing elsewhere, except when under the effects of Levioso. Diricawl eggs were green in colour.
