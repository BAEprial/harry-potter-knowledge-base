# 炸尾螺

> 英文名：Blast-Ended Skrewt

## 条目内容（英文原文）

- **简介**：The Blast-Ended Skrewt was a magical hybrid beast bred by Rubeus Hagrid in the autumn of 1994 by cross-breeding Manticores and Fire Crabs. Whether Hagrid used magic or somehow got the two different species to mate remains unknown.
- **原产地**：Hogwarts Castle
- **特征**：Resemble pale, slimy, deformed shell-less lobsters or scorpions；Covered in grey, shiny armour；Males have stingers, females have suckers to suck blood；Aggressive temperament and repulsive appearance；Shoot fire from their rear end
- **相关**：Fire Crab；Manticore
- **状态**：Unknown, possibly extinct
