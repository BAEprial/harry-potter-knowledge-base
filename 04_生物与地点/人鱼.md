# 人鱼

> 英文名：Merperson

## 条目内容（英文原文）

- **简介**：A merperson (plural merpeople) was a sentient magical beast that lived underwater. They were found all over the world. Their customs and habits were mysterious, and, like the centaurs, were offered the being status but refused in favour of beast, as they did not want to be placed in a status with hags and vampires. Merpeople could breathe above the water for a time, but it was unclear if they could ever truly leave their habitat.
