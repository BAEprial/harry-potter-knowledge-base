| 概述 | Glow Bugs possessed a light organ called a "blinker",，which allowed them to produce repeated,，bright flashes. While these flashes were usually random,，certain patterns could be used to convey information between individuals. Glow Bugs were intelligent enough to communicate with humans through these flashes,，ceasing their light if a human successfully interpreted the message they were trying to send. However,，their glow could also attract predators,，as some fish species were highly sensitive to a Glow Bug's light. For this reason,，Glow Bugs were sometimes used as bait by wizarding fishers. |
