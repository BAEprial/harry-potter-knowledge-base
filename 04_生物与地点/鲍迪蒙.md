# 鲍迪蒙

> 英文名：Bundimun

## 条目内容（英文原文）

- **简介**：It was a greenish, many-eyed pest that fed on dirt. The creature crept under floorboards and behind skirting boards, infesting houses. Their presence was indicated by the foul stench of decay. The secretions of the Bundimun were extremely acidic and could rot building structures. A large enough infestation could cause a building to collapse as the foundations were rotted away. Scouring Charms could clear out small infestations, but large colonies needed to be dealt with by the Department for the Regulation and Control of Magical Creatures (Pest Sub-Division). Bundimuns spat out its acidic ooze when annoyed, but when diluted, were used in some magical cleaning solutions.
