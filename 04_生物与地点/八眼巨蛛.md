## 条目内容（英文原文）

- **简介**：An Acromantula was a giant magical species of spider native to the rainforests of Southeast Asia, particularly Borneo, where it inhabited dense jungles. Acromantulas were believed to be a wizard-bred species, designed to guard dwellings or treasure hoards, and were first created before the Ban on Experimental Breeding in 1965. These giant spiders had a taste for human flesh and were first spotted in 1794. Despite their danger to wizardkind, there existed at least one group dedicated to protecting the species, the Société de Protection des Acromantules, based in the Place Cachée in Paris.
