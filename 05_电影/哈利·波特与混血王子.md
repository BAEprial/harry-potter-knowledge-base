# 哈利·波特与混血王子

> 英文名：Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince

## 概要

The sixth installment in the Harry Potter series begins with Harry accompanying Dumbledore to persuade Horace Slughorn to return to teaching at Hogwarts. Harry suspects Malfoy is now a Death Eater and is determined to find out the truth. Harry eventually becomes Slughorn’s favorite student upon finding a book belonging to the “Half-Blood Prince”. Using this connection, Dumbledore successfully obtains a memory from Slughorn linking to a young Voldemort and his inquiries about Horcruxes. Harry and Dumbledore venture out to find and destroy a Horcrux unaware of the grave fate that awaits them.
