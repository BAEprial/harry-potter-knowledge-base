Jackdaw's Clue is a Quest Item in . It is exclusive to players in House Hufflepuff.

## Description（英文原文）

:A clue from Richard Jackdaw to Anne Thisbe

## Text（英文原文）

:My dearest Anne – :Well done! You solved my puzzle. I knew you were a kindred spirit and the only one with whom I could truly share my adventures. Meet me at the edge of the Forbidden Forest as soon as you can and I will show you the map that I mentioned. There is no telling where it will lead us. I found it on pages that Peeves ripped from a secret book – and no one else seems to be able to find. I haven't any idea what lies ahead, but I am glad to know I shall share my future with you.This is only the beginning. :Richard :P.S. I am terribly glad you found this, as I would have gone on alone if you did not. And what fun would that be?
