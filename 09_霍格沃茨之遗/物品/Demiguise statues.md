# Demiguise statues

> 英文名：Demiguise statues


## 简介（英文原文）

Demiguise statues are small statues of a Demiguise holding a spherical moon. During the day the statue is bronze and the moon has an opalescent blue sheen. At night the statue becomes transparent with a brilliant blue-white moon; in this state it is possible to remove the moon, which causes the statue to disappear. This helps the player during the quest The Man Behind the Moons to get Alohomora I, II and III.
