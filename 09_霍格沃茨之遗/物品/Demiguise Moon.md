# Demiguise Moon

> 英文名：Demiguise Moon


## 简介（英文原文）

A Demiguise Moon is a quest item in . It is part of a Demiguise statue and can only be removed at night, which causes the statue to disappear. A large number of them must be collected for the quests The Caretaker's Lunar Lament and The Man Behind the Moons.
