## Uses（英文原文）

Wiggenweld Potion is an essential recipe to learn, as it restores health and allows the player to regenerate health faster, should they take damage in combat. On higher difficulty levels, Wiggenweld Potion is oftentimes the only source of healing for players. It is essential to stock up on as many as possible before entering tough battles against enemies.

## Locations（英文原文）

Wiggenweld vials may be bought from various shops, with the most accessible being J. Pippin's Potions in Hogsmeade. Players can also brew their own Wiggenweld Potion in the Potions Classroom at Hogwarts, or at their own Potions Station in the Room of Requirement. Wiggenweld requires Horklump juice and Dittany leaves to brew.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

The potion can be upgraded with the Wiggenweld Potency I and Wiggenweld Potency II talents.
