# Centaurs

> 英文名：Centaurs


## Introduction（英文原文）

Centaurs are highly intelligent magical beings inhabiting the forests of the Wizarding World. Known for their deep connection to nature and their advanced knowledge of astronomy and divination, centaurs play a discreet but significant role in the world surrounding Hogwarts Legacy. In the game, centaurs are primarily found in the Forbidden Forest, where they live according to their own traditions, separate from both wizard and goblin societies.

## Overview（英文原文）

Centaurs possess the upper body of a human and the lower body of a horse. They are known for their strength, speed, and wisdom, but also for their strong sense of independence. They do not consider themselves under the authority of the Ministry of Magic and generally avoid direct interaction with witches and wizards unless absolutely necessary. In Hogwarts Legacy, centaurs are portrayed as observant and cautious beings who closely monitor events that may affect the balance of nature.

## Habitat（英文原文）

Centaurs reside primarily in the Forbidden Forest, a vast and dangerous woodland located near Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. This environment provides: * Dense vegetation for shelter * Magical creatures for coexistence * Isolation from human interference The forest is considered their territory, and intrusions are often met with suspicion.

## Culture and Beliefs（英文原文）

Centaurs are deeply connected to the stars and practice a form of celestial observation often associated with Divination. They believe that: * The stars can reveal patterns of fate * Events are part of a greater cosmic order * Interference with destiny is dangerous Because of this, they tend to remain neutral in conflicts, even when major events unfold within the Wizarding World.

## Role in Hogwarts Legacy（英文原文）

In Hogwarts Legacy, centaurs appear as part of the wider ecosystem of the game world rather than as central characters. Their role includes: * Enhancing world immersion * Reinforcing the lore of the Wizarding World * Representing ancient magical traditions They may react to player presence, especially within the Forbidden Forest, but typically avoid direct involvement unless provoked.

## Relations with Wizards（英文原文）

Relations between centaurs and wizards have historically been tense. Although the Ministry of Magic officially classifies them as "Beasts," centaurs consider themselves equal or superior to humans. This classification has contributed to their mistrust. At times, individual centaurs may cooperate with humans, but such cases remain rare.

## Notable Traits（英文原文）

* Exceptional physical strength and agility * Advanced knowledge of Divination and astronomy * Strong independence and pride * Deep respect for nature * Reluctance to engage in human conflicts

## Behind the Scenes（英文原文）

Centaurs are a long-established part of the Wizarding World, originally introduced in the Harry Potter series. In Hogwarts Legacy, they serve to maintain continuity with existing lore while enriching the game’s world-building and environmental storytelling.
