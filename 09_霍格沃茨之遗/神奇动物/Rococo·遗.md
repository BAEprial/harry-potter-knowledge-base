## Biography（英文原文）

Rococo became the pet of Agnes Coffey at an unknown date. One day, as Coffey took Rococo on a walk by Henrietta's Hideaway, Rococo spotted something shiny reflect light from the sunrise through a window, and ran into the hideaway to get it. Rococo was eventually brought back home to Coffey by an student.
