## Beast Dens（英文原文）

Across the open world, you will find Beast Dens scattered about. These beast dens can carry around one to fifteen of one type of Beast, depending on rarity. You can capture these Beasts by using the Nab-Sack on them. ===Shiny Dens=== Shiny Dens are a type of Beast Den that is comprised primarily of Shiny Beasts. So far there are two known Shiny Dens scattered across the world. Both of which are in Cragcroftshire. Hogwarts Legacy Shiny Den Locations

## Beast Care（英文原文）

Beast Care is a feature in which you take care of beasts to protect them from the poachers of the world. By taking care of the beasts, magical materials will be released that can be woven into your clothes using the loom. You take care of these beasts by feeding and grooming them. When you do so, you get to collect 3-5 of the beast's product, depending on the rarity of the beasts. These products can then be used to upgrade and apply Traits to your armor. You can automatically feed the beasts by using a Beast Feeder. es:Criaturas
