## Notes（英文原文）

* You may be able to find a higher than average amount of special white nifflers at the den due south of Cragcroft. * Nifflers do not get along with Hippogriffs if they are placed in the same vivarium. However this does not affect the amount of materials players nurture from either beasts. * Nifflers are gold-storing creatures and are excellent to keep in your vivarium.
