## Description（英文原文）

The faculty tower can be unlocked when you learn Alohomora in the quest The Caretaker's Lunar Lament. You must complete the quest before being allowed to explore freely. It includes a Daedalian Key and an Arithmancy Door. The Faculty Tower contains the following notes: * Letter from Sirona Ryan * Letter to Fig from the Minister * Wizarding Crackers Notice * Letter from Ronen's Wife * Ronen's Lesson Plan * Letter from a Publisher * Note from Miriam * Letter from a Friend
