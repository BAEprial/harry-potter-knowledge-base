# Vivarium

> 英文名：Vivarium


## 简介（英文原文）

A Vivarium is an extension of the Room of Requirement in which rescued Beasts can be kept and cared for. Each vivarium replicates a particular environment and can hold up to 12 beasts and up to 4 species.

## List of vivariums（英文原文）

; Forest Vivarium : A grassy clearing in a forest with a small stream and waterfall, surrounded by cliffs and distant mountains. This vivarium is added at the end of The Elf, the Nab-Sack, and the Loom. ; Coastal Vivarium : A beach on the coast, surrounded by rocky cliffs. This vivarium is added after completing the side quest The Plight of the House-Elf. ; Swamp Vivarium : A dimly lit swamp filled with a light fog. This vivarium is added after completing Foal of the Dead. ; Mountain Vivarium : This vivarium is added after Phoenix Rising.
