# Hospital Wing

> 英文名：Hospital Wing


## 简介（英文原文）

The Hospital Wing is located within the Faculty Tower, which is part of the South Wing of Hogwarts Castle.

## Description（英文原文）

You can access the Hospital Wing after you complete the quest The Caretaker's Lunar Lament and will need to be able to access the room in the quest Acting on Instinct. You can find the Vanishing Sickness Record and Quidditch Injury Record in the room.
