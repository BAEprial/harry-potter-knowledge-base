# Hogsmeade Square

> 英文名：Hogsmeade Square


## 简介（英文原文）

Hogsmeade Square is is the main square of the village of Hogsmeade. It is bordered by Gladrags Wizardwear, Dervish and Banges, Honeydukes, Steepley and Sons and the Hogsmeade Post Office. A statue of Hengist of Woodcroft can be found in the centre.
