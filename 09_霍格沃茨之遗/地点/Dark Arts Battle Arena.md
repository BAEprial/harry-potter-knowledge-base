> 英文名：Dark Arts Battle Arena


## 简介（英文原文）

Dark Arts Battle Arena is one of the locations in the game . It is found in The Forbidden Forest.

## Description（英文原文）

Fight waves of enemies in this Battle Arena with the Unforgiveable Curses, Avadra Kedavra, Imperio, and Crucio, as well as the Blasting Curse, Confringo. In addition to their unique and devastating effects, Unforgiveable Curses break through all Shield Charms.
