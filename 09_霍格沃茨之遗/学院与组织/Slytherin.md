## Dormitory Notes（英文原文）

Directly across from the player's dorm, a 6th year room, there is a small chest, at the end of the bed to the player's immediate right, will reward gold. Working counter-clockwise, the 2nd year dorm will contain a small chest, against the opposite wall, rewarding gold. Once out of this hall, going up the center stairwell that splits to the left and right, going left, the room contains a large chest that is locked by a level 2 lock. Going behind the statue, in a small alcove, a chest locked by a level 3 lock is hidden. Turning right after descending the forked stairwell will lead to more dormitories. Proceeding to the 6th year dorm room, there will be a level 1 locked chest next to the bed at the player's immediate right. The Slytherin House Chest, unlocked by tokens earned by finding all 16 Daedalian keys, is located at the top of the spiral staircase leading to the Slytherin entrance.
