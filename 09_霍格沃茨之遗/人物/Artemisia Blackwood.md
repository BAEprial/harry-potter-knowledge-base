# Artemisia Blackwood

> 英文名：Artemisia Blackwood


## 简介（英文原文）

Artemisia Blackwood (née Black) was a disowned, half-blood member of the Black family. Due to this, she developed a hatred towards muggles.

## Biography（英文原文）

Artemisia Black's mother was part of the pure-blood supremacist Black family and so when she had a dalliance with a muggle man that resulted in Artemisia, Artemisia was ostracized and disowned by the Black family. As a result, Artemisia blamed her muggle father for this ostracization. When she started to attend Hogwarts, she met a muggle-born student, Eunon Wood. Despite this, she didn't blame him for his heritage, viewing Eunon as an unwilling, innocent victim of muggle impurity. With Eunon likewise having a contentious relationship with muggles via his intolerant parents, Eunon and Artemisia grew close. Artemisia had a major influence not just on Eunon's growth but also his descent into darkness as the two fed on each other's disdain for their muggle heritage. The two eventually wed, though rather oddly had invited their muggle families to the wedding. Eunon's family in particular would have avoided it altogether if not for the rumors of Artemisia's large dowry, which unbeknownst to the muggles, was really just a single gold medallion that was bewitched to look like a pile of gold. To celebrate their union, Eunon and Artemisia combined their last names into "Blackwood" and Eunon
