## Biography（英文原文）

Aidan was a friend of Sirona Ryan's father and he, along with his wife Dorothy, opened up their home for Sirona to stay in during the summers she worked at The Three Broomsticks as a student. Around 1890, Dorothy was cleaning out a closet and found an old box of some of Sirona's letters from her school years. Aidan took the box, planning to take it with him when he next visited Sirona. He then headed out to scavenge Horklump Juice from a nearby cave for his wife's Wiggenweld Potion supply but he fell ill and dropped the box in the cave. While he made it home, he sadly never recovered and passed away a short time later.
