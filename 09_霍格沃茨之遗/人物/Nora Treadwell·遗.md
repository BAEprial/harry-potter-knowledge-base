# Nora Treadwell·遗

> 英文名：Nora Treadwell


## 简介（英文原文）

Nora Treadwell is an archaeologist and studies Merlin's mysterious structures around Hogwarts.

## Biography（英文原文）

She attended Hogwarts where she met her future wife, Priya. Priya was also the one who first got Nora hooked on learning about Merlin after Priya gifted her a book about the famous wizard. After graduating, the two got married and Nora spent her time trying to research more about Merlin's mysterious puzzle structures, deducing it had something to do with mallowsweet. As she was researching at one location in Lower Hogsfield to test her theory, several of Victor Rookwood's henchmen accosted her, demanding she give them info on a student she didn't know anything about just as the student in question showed up. When the henchmen made it clear they were going to try and take Nora out regardless, she paired up with the student to dispatch of the henchmen. After the threats were taken care of, she filled in the student about her latest theory and had them help her out, soon revealing Merlin's mysterious structures were puzzles that, when solved, created a stone arbor. Pleased with the information she had for her research, she then headed off to write down all her findings.
