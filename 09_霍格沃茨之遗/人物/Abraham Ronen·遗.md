## Description（英文原文）

:Jovial and lighthearted with a perpetual twinkle in his eye, Professor Ronen considers his students' friendships and extracurricular activities to be just as important as the subject of Charms itself. In response to having been tutored by a rather austere father before and during his years at Hogwarts, he developed a passion for games of all forms - particularly Gobstones. (He allegedly met his wife during an especially lively Gobstones tournament.) To the delight of most of his students, his unorthodox teaching style often incorporates gameplay.

## Biography（英文原文）

When he was young, Abraham was tutored by a rather strict father both before and during his time at Hogwarts, where he was sorted into Slytherin. Due to this strict upbringing, Abraham turned to playing games, taking a particular interest in gobstones and eventually meeting his wife at a gobstones tournament. By 1883, he started teaching at Hogwarts as the Charms Professor and incorporated game activities into his lessons to teach his students. By 1890, he tried to also give pointers to some of his colleagues, particularly Cuthbert Binns who had noted students appeared to be lethargic in his classes, something Abraham never saw in his own.
