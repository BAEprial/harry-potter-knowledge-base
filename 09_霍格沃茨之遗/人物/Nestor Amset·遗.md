## Biography（英文原文）

When he was alive, Sir Nestor took part in a plot to overthrow his county's government and take power, but ended up choosing the losing side in the dispute and was subsequently executed via beheading sometime prior to 1890. He later came back as a ghost and became a member of the Headless Hunt, albeit their lowest ranking member. In 1890, he participates in a Headless Hunt party at the Hogsmeade Graveyard.
