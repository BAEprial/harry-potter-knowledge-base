## Biography（英文原文）

As she ran a beast shop, Ellie used to sell a variety of related beast products, including magical creatures. However, this didn't come without grievances, such as diricawl buyers wanting their money back as the birds tended to disappear, fwooper feather suppliers going mad from the bird's cries and ending up in St. Mungo's, and even parents getting annoyed if their child so much as caught a flea off a kneazle. Combined with her wanting to make sure any creature she sold was well cared for and in a safe home, she decided to stop selling creatures and instead just sell their byproducts.
