## Biography（英文原文）

Tobbs worked with Deek and several other house-elves for a master who was a cruel poacher. When their master died, as he had no kin, all his house-elves were re-assigned by the Ministry of Magic. Deek was sent off to Hogwarts but Tobbs wasn't so lucky and ended up serving another cruel master. Despite Tobbs' fear of spiders, his master forced him to stay in a spider-infested cave to gather him Leech Juice for his business. If Tobbs failed to meet his given quota, his master would punish him by refusing him food for the week while still expecting Tobbs to punish himself. In 1890, Tobbs couldn't find anymore leeches in the first area, so he moved deeper into the cave. Unfortunately, this was also home to a large acromantula, which killed Tobbs before he could finish writing one last letter to his master.
