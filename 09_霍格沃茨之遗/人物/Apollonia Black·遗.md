# Apollonia Black·遗

> 英文名：Apollonia Black


## 简介（英文原文）

Apollonia Black was a witch from the noble House of Black and a contemporary of Phineas Nigellus Black. She attended Hogwarts in the 19th century and owned a house-elf named Scrope.

## Biography（英文原文）

Richard Jackdaw tried to win her affection by offering pages stolen from Peeves, but she rejected him. Out of jealousy or prejudice, Apollonia falsely testified against Jackdaw’s lover, Anne Thisbe, a Muggle-born, leading to Anne’s wrongful imprisonment in Azkaban — helped by the Black family's influence. Apollonia became obsessed with the stolen pages and hid them in her private grotto, which she forbade Scrope from entering. She once injured him with a cleaver and kept a family ring there, later sold by Jackdaw. She also owned a journal. She died in a delirious state after mistakenly eating poisonous belladonna, despite Scrope’s warnings.
