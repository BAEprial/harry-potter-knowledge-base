## Biography（英文原文）

Anne, alongside her twin brother Sebastian, were born to two professors who instilled in their children a thirst for knowledge. However, while Anne and Sebastian were still quite young, they heard a crash from the cellar room where their parents often went to read over books. They ran down to investigate, only to find their parents dead from a defective lamp that caused the room to fill with an undetectable toxin. Their uncle, Solomon, then took the twins into his home at Feldcroft. === Hogwarts === When they were eleven, Anne and Sebastian started to attend Hogwarts, where both were sorted into Slytherin, and they soon became close friends with their fellow Slytherin, Ominis Gaunt.
