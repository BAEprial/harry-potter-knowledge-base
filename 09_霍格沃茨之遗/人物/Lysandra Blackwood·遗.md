# Lysandra Blackwood·遗

> 英文名：Lysandra Blackwood


## 简介（英文原文）

Lysandra Blackwood was a squib and the only child of Artemisia and Eunon Blackwood.

## Biography（英文原文）

Lysandra was born to magical parents but when it became apparent Lysandra lacked any magical abilities and was thus a squib, her blood-purist parents viewed her as a disappointment. This disappointment would culminate over the years, causing a lot of frustration between her and her parents and it all came to a head when she was 16. After one particularly bad fight, Lysandra went and reported her mother to some muggle witch hunters who then came for Artemisia and burned her at the stake. After that, Lysandra and her father both disappeared, with neither one ever being seen again.

## Notes（英文原文）

* Considering the lore given indicates Lysandra became frustrated with both of her parents, it's unclear why she only reported her mother to the witch hunters rather than both of them.
