## Biography（英文原文）

Helen grew up with her brother Eddie in Brocburrow but while her brother stayed in their hamlet and went on to run a small shop there, Helen decided she wanted to become an Auror and after graduating, left for the Ministry in London. She had a fulfilling career there as an Auror, though had some cases she was never able to really solve. In particular, when the ghost of a Hogwarts student, Richard Jackdaw, suddenly appeared but then ran off, Apollonia Black accused under the flimsiest of excuses that Richard's romantic interest, Anne Thisbe, had done it. The Black family's name, combined with Anne being a muggle-born, all but sealed her fate and Anne was sentenced to Azkaban and she went on to blame Helen for not proving her innocence. Years later, Helen was eventually forced into an early retirement and she moved back to the general Hogwarts area, settling down in Upper Hogsfield. She began to write up her memoirs of her life as an Auror while attempting to take up gardening but eventually realized that too much time on her hands made her dwell on the past and possible mistakes she made in her career. She then resolved to find another hobby as gardening proved not to be her thing.
