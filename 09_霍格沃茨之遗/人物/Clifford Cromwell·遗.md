## Biography（英文原文）

Clifford attended Hogwarts, where he was sorted into Hufflepuff House. After he graduated, he settled down in Marunweem while he worked for the Ministry of Magic on confidential projects, such as trying to use mandrakes as weapons. Eventually wanting a quieter life, he left the Ministry and setup a plant shop in Marunweem.
