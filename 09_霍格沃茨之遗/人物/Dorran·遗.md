## Biography（英文原文）

Around 1890, Dorran was part of the Forbidden Forest centaur herd that was led by Elek. However, Elek didn't see eye to eye with Dorran, often accusing Dorran as being out of touch with his old ways. When Poppy and the new fifth-year Hogwarts student appeared in the forest to try and get the centaurs' help in finding and protecting the Golden Snidgets from being found by the poachers, Elek refused to listen and nearly had the students killed before Dorran stepped in. While Elek did as asked, he threatened to kill Dorran if Dorran got in his way again before leaving. Dorran then chastised the students for being so reckless and ordered them to follow him so he could escort them safely out of the forest but stopped when Poppy quickly filled him in about the snidgets and that poachers were after them and the only clue they had was that it "lies in the moonlight." Realizing this might be related to a cryptic vision he saw many years ago, Dorran then told them to find a moonstone in a cave to the south and to take the moonstone to a henge in the forest.
