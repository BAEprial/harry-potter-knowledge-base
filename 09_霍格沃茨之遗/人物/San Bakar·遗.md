## Biography（英文原文）

=== Early life === Bakar was born in Yemen but once he learned of his magical abilities, he left his Muggle community and made his way to Hogwarts, where he was sorted into Slytherin. Sometime after he graduated, he became the Beasts Professor at his alma mater. === Adult life === At one point he was made aware of Ancient Magic and, alongside Headmistress Niamh Fitzgerald and Professor Charles Rookwood, joined Professor Percival Rackham as a group known as the Keepers, with their focus on guarding ancient magic and that which Rackham could see and utilize. While their primary purpose was to safeguard it, they also used it when possible to save others. In one such case, the group went to Feldcroft, a small hamlet that was suffering from a drought, and brought it back to life with ancient magic. Years later, Bakar and the rest of the Keepers were informed by Rookwood that their new fifth-year student, Isidora Morganach, had asked him about the strange swirls she saw that day they saved her hamlet.
