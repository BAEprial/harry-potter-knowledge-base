## Biography（英文原文）

In the late 19th century, he and his wife, Daisy, both worked at Gringotts, and were friends with the Bickles. One day, Theophilus Harlow approached his wife to try and get her to help him access the treasure in the bank's vaults, but she refused to assist him, and believed that was the end of it. Instead, Harlow had Isko kidnapped, trampling his prized flowers in the process, and held him in a cell in the Ashwinder's hideout beneath Hog's Head in the hopes of getting Daisy to "help" them. Isko's wand was taken from him and hidden so he couldn't use it to free himself. After being found and given his wand by Hogwarts' new fifth-year student, he disapparated and found Ruth Singer and informed the auror what had transpired.
