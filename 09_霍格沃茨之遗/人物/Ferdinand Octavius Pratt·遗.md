## Biography（英文原文）

Ferdinand attended Hogwarts where, while he was a student, he was a prefect. By 1890 there were two known portraits of him: one in Hogwarts' library and the other at The Three Broomsticks. Headmaster Phineas Nigellus Black used this to his advantage, asking Ferdinand to spy on students at The Three Broomsticks and report back to him and Ferdinand readily agreed to this task. However, if he had nothing interesting to report, he'd instead make up rumors, causing many students to be unfairly punished, like Astoria Crickett. Finally fed up with Ferdinand's spying and lying, Astoria had some Ashwinders steal his frame out of The Three Broomsticks.
