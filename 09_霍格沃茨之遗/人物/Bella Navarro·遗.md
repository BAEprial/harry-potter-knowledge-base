## Biography（英文原文）

Bella was born and raised in Cragcroft, but at one point moved to Siena, Italy, where she lived for several decades. She only returned to Cragcroft recently in the late 19th century after deciding there was nothing left for her in Siena and that she needed a change of scenery. However, even on returning to Cragcroft, she ran into trouble in the form of the Ashwinders, who were disrupting trade routes in the area.
