## Biography（英文原文）

===Early life=== During his youth, Ranrok one day came across an illegal dragon-breeding camp run by wizards and was fascinated by the creatures. He'd then visit the camp in secret whenever he could to see them before finally mustering the courage after several weeks to speak to the wizard in charge as he wished to join them. Ranrok saw his chance at introductions when he noticed the wizard had dropped his wand and proceeded to pick it up to hand it back to the wizard. However, seeing a goblin with a wand, which had been made illegal in the 1600s, sent the wizard into a rage and he assaulted Ranrok to the point of nearly killing him. This event left Ranrok with a hatred for wizards. ===Ranrok's rebellion=== By the late 19th century, Ranrok had gained enough traction that he had a sizable group of followers, Ranrok's Loyalists, who spread mayhem throughout the Scottish Highlands. During one of his searches, he had his younger brother, Lodgok, go collect some of their ancestor Bragbor's journals, which revealed that Bragbor had been commissioned to build some repositories for several talented witches and wizards: Rackham, Fitzgerald, Bakar, Morganach, and Rookwood.
