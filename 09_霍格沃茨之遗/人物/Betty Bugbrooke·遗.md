## Biography（英文原文）

At one point, Betty ran into and befriended a young unicorn foal, Hazel. The two formed a friendship of sorts and for years, Betty would go check on Hazel in the Forbidden Forest once a week to bring her treats and brush her coat. However, during their last encounter, they were attacked by wolves and Hazel was injured trying to protect Betty. Hazel then went into hiding and Betty worried poachers might go after and kill Hazel for her blood and so sought someone out to find and take Hazel to a safe place.
