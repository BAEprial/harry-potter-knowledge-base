## Biography（英文原文）

Fitzgerald attended Hogwarts, where she was sorted into Ravenclaw. Eventually she became the school's Headmistress. At one point, she, along with her colleagues Charles Rookwood and San Bakar, joined Percival Rackham as a group known as the Keepers, who prioritized safeguarding Ancient Magic and which Rackham was able to see and utilize. While they were mostly focused on keeping it a secret, when they could, they'd use it to save others, which they did one day by going to a drought-stricken hamlet, Feldcroft, and reviving it. === Meeting Isidora === Years after saving Feldcroft, the group was informed by Rookwood he had been approached by a new fifth-year student, Isidora Morganach, asking about the strange swirls she saw as as child that day they saved her hamlet. The group then met with Isidora and informed her about them being Keepers and that Rackham could train her on how to use ancient magic. Sometime after Isidora had graduated and started to travel around, the Defence Against the Dark Arts teaching position opened up and Isidora accepted the position. Fitzgerald and the rest of the Keepers warmly welcomed her back from her travels and where they were invited back to Isidora
