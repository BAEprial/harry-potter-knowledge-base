## Biography（英文原文）

Despite the traditional jobs goblins took, Arn found himself drawn to painting and was able to make a career out of it. However, Ranrok's loyalists, who saw any goblin that didn't join them as being a traitor, attacked Arn when he refused to join them and took all his paintings and tools.
