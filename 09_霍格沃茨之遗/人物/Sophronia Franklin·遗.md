## Biography（英文原文）

Sophronia loved all kinds of knowledge, even small bits that most considered trivia, and made a quiz out of the information. However, none of the other students seemed interested in this, so she instead got a new fifth-year to take it in return for a book they needed for class. That same year, she learned of the famed witch Herodiana Byrne, considered the greatest Depulso master of all time. Herodiana herself taught at Hogwarts for a short time, creating a special puzzle room that could only be accessed with her signature spell and Sophronia wished to access it to see Herodiana's outfit, but as a third-year, she hadn't been taught the required spell yet. Thus, she sought out an older student to assist in recovering the outfit so she could view it.
