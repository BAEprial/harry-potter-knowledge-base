## Biography（英文原文）

Priya attended Hogwarts, where she was sorted into Hufflepuff and met her future wife, Nora, there. While students, she gifted Nora a book on Merlin and Nora became hooked on researching about the famed wizard. After they graduated, the two got married and Priya went into potions, making and selling them and even owning her own shop in Knockturn Alley. Despite having her own stationary shop, Priya also would wander around as a traveling vendor in the Highlands which in turn kept her up to date about the latest news and dangers going on.
