## Biography（英文原文）

Deek originally worked for a cruel poacher alongside several other house-elves, like Tobbs, and where he saw them put to use nab-sacks to capture beasts. One day, his master ordered Deek to help him capture a phoenix off a high cliff. Deek begged his master to leave the phoenix be, but was still ordered to go get the bird. When Deek hesitated climbing up, he had to punish himself and as he did so, Deek's master only grew angrier and angrier until he cast at the bird himself. The phoenix, which had been guarding her nest, swooped down in a fury and, before Deek could get to him, his master fell to his death. Deek stayed there for days on the cliffside, punishing himself, before Tobbs found him. As his master didn't have any next of kin, with his passing Deek, Tobbs, and the rest were then assigned out by the Ministry of Magic. Deek was sent to work for Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry which he enjoyed a lot more, though was saddened to see Tobbs wasn't also assigned to the school. He later befriended a young Matilda Weasley during her second year at Hogwarts and the two discovered the Room of Requirement together.
