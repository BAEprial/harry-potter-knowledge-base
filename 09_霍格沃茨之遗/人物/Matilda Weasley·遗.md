## Description（英文原文）

:Matilda Weasley is an exceptionally talented witch with a background as a cursebreaker for the Ministry - known for her composure and extraordinary wandwork. She is a firm but fair Transfiguration Professor and a slightly intimidating, well-respected Deputy Headmistress. Professor Weasley has been tasked with getting the player up to speed in their studies and introduces them to their Wizard's Field Guide and the Room of Requirement - both of which are invaluable resources for a new fifth-year with a great deal to learn.

## Biography（英文原文）

Matilda attended Hogwarts where, in her second year, she met Deek, one of Hogwarts' house elves, and the two became friends and eventually discovered the Room of Requirement together. After she graduated, she went on to work as a cursebreaker for the Ministry of Magic and eventually, as part of her job, she traveled the world, working to share magical knowledge between countries. During this same time period she met Chiyo Kogawa, an expert flyer and a Japanese Ministry of Magic member, near Yokohama harbor. Matilda's Ministry work ended up making her well known enough around the world that many had heard of her, even if they never personally met her. She eventually left her job at the Ministry to become the Transfiguration professor at her old school, and was later appointed as Deputy Headmistress under Headmaster Phineas Nigellus Black, who pushed off most of his duties to Matilda, like hiring new staff. As such, when a position opened up for a Divination professor at Hogwarts, she reached out to Mudiwa Onai, who she had heard about and knew taught Divination at Uagadou, and Mudiwa readily accepted.
