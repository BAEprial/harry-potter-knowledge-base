## Biography（英文原文）

Suchrita grew up with her sister Satyavati in a mostly Muggle world, though they came from a magical family. However, it soon became apparent that Suchrita was a Squib, the first in several generations in their family. Despite this, the two sisters managed to bond over stargazing and would spend countless nights doing so so on their home's rooftop. While Suchrita was initially disappointed she couldn't attend Hogwarts with her sister, she found herself excelling on her own in Muggle schools, even attending Cambridge University, albeit disguised as a gentleman thanks to her mother's magic, and continued to use this male pseudonym to contribute to astronomy in the Muggle world. She also remained close with her sister, with the two still stargazing and taking trips together.
