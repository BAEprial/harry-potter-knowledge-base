> 英文名：Johanna Bickle


## 简介（英文原文）

Johanna Bickle was a witch living in Lower Hogsfield with her family in the late 19th century in .

## Biography（英文原文）

Johanna lived with her husband and their son, Archie, in Lower Hogsfield, but in the late 19th century, Theophilus Harlow, second-in-command of the Rookwood Gang, was harassing a lot of locals. Johanna's husband eventually got fed up with it and began to look into Harlow's dealings and discovered some evidence that he turned over to Auror Ruth Singer. However, while this wasn't enough for Singer to arrest Harlow, it was enough to catch Harlow's attention. Harlow began to threaten Johanna's husband but this apparently wasn't enough to dissuade him. In 1890, Johanna came home to discover her husband's dead body and realized Harlow had killed him, and on top of that Archie was missing. She became distraught over this, hoping maybe Archie hadn't been taken away and would return home safely.
