## Biography（英文原文）

Sirona attended Hogwarts, where she was sorted into Ravenclaw and made friends everywhere she went for her sincere and unapologetic self, such as standing up for fellow classmates and reaching out to those who felt they didn't belong. While attending school, she also joined her House's Quidditch team, playing as seeker for three years. To help pay for school related costs, she worked by waiting on tables at the Three Broomsticks and, during the summers, stayed with a couple in the area, Aidan and Dorothy Sprottle. While working during her seventh year, she instinctively caught onto a first-year Mirabel having a hard time fitting in and reached out to her, with the two forming a long-lasting friendship as a result. Sirona, having loved the work and interactions she got at the Three Broomsticks, set her sights on one day owning such a place, and eventually by the late 19th century, came to own the very bar she worked at during her school years.
