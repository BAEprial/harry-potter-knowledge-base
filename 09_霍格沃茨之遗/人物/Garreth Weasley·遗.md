## Description（英文原文）

:An easygoing, popular boy, Garreth Weasley is a talented student. However, he rarely devotes much time to his schoolwork, instead choosing to experiment with his own brews of magical drinks or relaxing on the school grounds with his friends. He loves his aunt Matilda, Deputy Headmisstress, but is tired of being under her watchful eye. He just may ask for your help in procuring hard-to-find ingredients for his creative concoctions.

## Biography（英文原文）

Garreth loved to experiment and create his own magical drinks, using his family as unwitting test subjects for his various attempts to the point his father was happy when he headed off to school as he could then bug his fellow students to test his drinks out instead. When he headed off to Hogwarts, he was sorted into Gryffindor but with his aunt, Matilda Weasley, being the Deputy Headmistress, he was kept under a watchful eye to make sure he was staying out of trouble. Despite this, by his fifth year, he was still trying out new drinks and enlisted the help of a new fifth-year to try and help him out during a potions class while seeking other ways to get the ingredients he needed. His experiments were not without their problems though, like where he caused a small fire in one classroom to his aunt's chagrin and who promptly gave her nephew detention. Unlike Amit Thakkar, but like many students like Adelaide Oakes: They both dislike Astronomy and find in boring. However it is also because both are intolerant to cold weather.
