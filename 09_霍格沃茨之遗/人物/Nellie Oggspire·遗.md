# Nellie Oggspire·遗

> 英文名：Nellie Oggspire


## 简介（英文原文）

Nellie Oggspire is a Gryffindor student at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. She is a member of the Crossed Wands duelling club.

## Biography（英文原文）

Nellie has been known to find herself on the top of various buildings and tall objects from a young age, like when she was five and her mother found that Nellie had somehow gotten on top of their roof. This continued into her school years at Hogwarts, where she was sorted into Gryffindor, and was noted to walk around on top of her common room's fireplace mantle and allegedly made it to the top of Gryffindor Tower without a broom.

## Involvement（英文原文）

Nellie provides the player with the side quest The Daedalian Keys. She is also an opponent in the third round of the Crossed Wands tournament.
