## Biography（英文原文）

From a young age, Rowland knew he wanted to be a metal trader and in turn developed an appreciation for goblin culture and learned Gobbledegook. As an adult, he was able to make his dream a reality and became well known in the goblin community for his metal trading. After his sibling and their partner passed away, leaving his 5-year-old niece Adelaide an orphan, he took her in and did his best to take care of her. When Adelaide started attending Hogwarts, Rowland traveled a lot for work to pay for both her school expenses and his taste for finer things, while sending his niece an owl every week about how he was faring. In 1890, Rowland decided to try and make contact with goblins that were part of Ranrok's Loyalists for business prospects, believing the money to be made was worth the risk.
