# Eunon Blackwood

> 英文名：Eunon Blackwood


## 简介（英文原文）

Eunon Blackwood (né Wood) was a muggle-born dark wizard known for creating the Blackwood Maze to ensnare and kill off muggles.

## Biography（英文原文）

Eunon Wood was born to a muggle family that worked as hedge trimmers and cultivators. However, Eunon was often misunderstood by his parents, so he sought solace by partaking in hedge trimming, where he could allegedly control bushes with his mind to form beautiful designs. This only led to his family being both impressed and infuriated with him, with their resentment worsening after Eunon got his Hogwarts invitation letter. At Hogwarts, he was sorted into Hufflepuff and where he first met Artemisia Black, a disowned member of the Black family due to her mother having a dalliance with Artemisia's muggle father. Eunon and Artemisia grew close, with Artemisia having a major influence on Eunon's growth, but also his descent into darkness as the two fed on each other's disdain for their muggle heritage. The two eventually wed, though rather oddly had invited their muggle families to the wedding. Eunon's family in particular would have avoided it altogether if not for the rumors of Artemisia's large dowry, which unbeknownst to the muggles, was really just a single gold medallion that was bewitched to look like a pile of gold.
