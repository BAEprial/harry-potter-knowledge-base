## Biography（英文原文）

Bernard attended Hogwarts, where he met and befriended Eddie Thistlewood, with the two remaining friends and business associates ever since. Though oddly enough, when speaking with Eddie, Mr. Thistlewood claims he has never left his hamlet, specifically mentioning that he had never attended Hogwarts. Having a fondness for working with beasts, Bernard decided to open a shop selling various beast byproducts and suspected his twin daughters would likely take over the shop one day as they also loved magical creatures. In 1890, Bernard was having ongoing problems with some of Ranrok's Loyalists in Feldcroft, with the increased presence making him suspect they were looking for something, though he had no clue what they were trying to find. At a loss on what to do about the increasing attacks, he wrote to his close friend Eddie Thistlewood for his input and Eddie devised to send a couple crates of Chinese Chomping Cabbages to Bernard to assist him against the goblins, but the crates got intercepted.
