## Biography（英文原文）

Charles attended Hogwarts, where he was sorted into Slytherin. He later became Hogwarts' Transfiguration Professor and Percival Rackham's closest friend. At one point, Rackham created a secret group known as the Keepers with Charles, Headmistress Niamh Fitzgerald, and fellow professor San Bakar, with their goal being to safeguard Ancient Magic. While they were trying to safeguard it, the Keepers also used it when they could to help out others, such as when they revived a drought ridden hamlet, Feldcroft. === Meeting Isidora === Years later, Charles was approached after class one day by a new fifth-year student, Isidora Morganach, who recognized him from the day he saved her hamlet. When she asked him about the odd, beautiful swirls she saw from that day, Charles realized she could also see ancient magic and informed the rest of the Keepers, who then asked Isidora to meet them in private. When she met with them all, they explained to her what she was seeing and that Rackham would be able to mentor her. Soon after Isidora graduated, she returned to Hogwarts, this time as the Defence Against the Dark Arts professor. Charles along with Rackham warmly welcomed her back from her travels
