## Biography（英文原文）

Richard attended Hogwarts and was known to be a heartbreaker. While he had shown interest in Apollonia Black, Apollonia showed no interest in him. He later on wooed and fell in love with Anne Thisbe. During his schooling, Richard worked as an assistant at Ollivanders' Hogsmeade shop but he mistook the Ollivanders' pride in their family heirloom wand as meaning the wand had special qualities and stole it and ran off. He soon learned it wasn't anymore special than a regular wand and intended to return it at a later time once he finished following a map he had "stolen" from Peeves. He invited Anne to join him by promising her adventure but only after she solved a series of puzzles he set up. However, she never showed up and he became upset, thinking she had blown him off. He continued on without her, following the map into the cave, but just as he got into the central part, his head was suddenly cut off, killing him instantly. Soon after, he returned as a ghost that was briefly seen in Hogsmeade, but then disappeared just as quickly from the area, traveling around the world to try and mend his broken heart over Anne. By the late 19th century, he returned to the area on a whim. :Prison
