## Biography（英文原文）

In 1886, Evangeline started at Hogwarts, where she was sorted into Hufflepuff and made friends with her fellow Hufflepuff, Adelaide Oakes. During Evie's fifth year, Headmaster Black orders the school's bells be removed as the sound of them is giving him a headache, Evangeline takes it upon herself to restore them as they are part of Hogwarts' history. However, being tone-deaf, she does not know what order to put them in, so she enlists the help of Adelaide. When the headmaster starts asking questions about what was going on, Adelaide grows uncomfortable with them going behind his back though and stops short of the last two bells being put back up. Evangeline then enlists the help of the new fifth-year student to put the last two bells up in the right order.
