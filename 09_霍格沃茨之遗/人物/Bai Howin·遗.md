## Description（英文原文）

:Bai Howin has a respect for beasts, but sees them for what they are: useful to wizardkind. An encounter in her youth with an Occamy left her in awe of beasts, and she loathes poachers who would waste them for Galleons. In her mind, magical creatures are to be valued for what they can provide for wizardkind: protective clothing, potion ingredients and even wand cores. It is her mission to ensure that the next generation of witches and wizards understands this.

## Biography（英文原文）

Bai attended Hogwarts, where she was sorted into Hufflepuff. During the summer before her seventh year, she learned of a wizard who'd been selling Occamy eggs and had gone missing in the mountains near her home. Having heard of occamy but never seen one, as they were native to places like India, a curious Bai headed out to see if she could find the elusive creature. Despite her parents' annoyance at her absence, Bai found the wizard's remains after a few days, near a tree hollow that contained an occamy nest. Despite the eggs having monetary value, Bai left them alone as she appreciated all the other byproducts a living creature could provide, rather than ending a creature's life just for coin. She then hid to see if the mother would appear and, soon enough, the beautiful creature did before shrinking down to fit into the hollow. It was at that moment Bai knew she wanted to better understand all beasts to ensure everyone learned to benefit properly from them.
