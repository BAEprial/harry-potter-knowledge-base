## Biography（英文原文）

Garnuff was born into a family where it was tradition to work for Gringotts, but he opted to go into activism instead, with a particular focus on beasts' rights. While he never considered himself one to have a pet, one day a mooncalf started following him and wouldn't leave his side. He eventually named her Biscuit and took such a liking to her that eventually he couldn't imagine being without her. However, in 1890, some members of the Poacher Pack took Biscuit and while Garnuff tried to fight back, he was no match for them and they ran off with the mooncalf. He then sought out help in getting Biscuit back and was answered by the new fifth-year Hogwarts student.
