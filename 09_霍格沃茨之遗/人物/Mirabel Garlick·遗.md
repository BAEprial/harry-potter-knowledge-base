## Description（英文原文）

:London-born Professor Garlick grew up in a household full of Muggles and didn't really feel at home until she was a student at Hogwarts. In fact, she will tell you that it was at the famed school of witchcraft and wizardry she finally 'blossomed' herself - and discovered her extraordinary affinity for magical plants. Her sunny demeanour and flowery language charm colleagues and students alike, prompting some students to worry she may one day leave them for another green-house. But Mirabel Garlick would never leave her students. So, even if the right witch were to come along, she'd simply have to love Hogwarts as much as Mirabel does.

## Biography（英文原文）

Garlick was a muggle-born witch, having grown up with her Muggle family along the Thames in London. When she was eleven, she received her invitation to Hogwarts, where she was then sorted into Hufflepuff. However, being a newcomer to the magical world and feeling alone at Hogwarts, Garlick originally had a hard time finding her place and pondered having her parents come fetch her before her first week of school was over. That all changed though when she met a seventh-year Sirona Ryan at The Three Broomsticks, who sensed Garlick was having a difficult time adjusting and reached out to her, forming a long-lasting friendship as as result. Garlick soon found her place at Hogwarts and began to feel at home, particularly with plants. She did so well that by her seventh year, she received an Award for Excellence for her skills in herbology. Soon after she graduated, she took over as Hogwarts' Herbology Professor as the previous professor ran into an unfortunate number of accidents in his last years of teaching, with his last endeavor involving a Venomous Tentacula putting him on an indefinite leave of absence.
