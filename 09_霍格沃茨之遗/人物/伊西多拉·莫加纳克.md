## Biography（英文原文）

===Early years=== When she was child, the small hamlet her family lived in was hit by a drought. However, a mysterious group appeared and quickly put an end to the drought, not only watering the land but making the plant life flourish once more. As they worked, Isidora saw beautiful, odd swirls all over the place, though her father just brushed it off as his daughter having a wild imagination. The group then left as quickly as they had appeared. Despite the hamlet being brought back to life, it was too late for her brother, who soon passed away.
