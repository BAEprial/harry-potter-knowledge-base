## Biography（英文原文）

Pádraic grew up in Irondale along with his sister, Catrin. However, his sister turned to a life of crime and left, leaving Pádraic horrified and heartbroken. In 1890, many in the hamlet, along with Pádraic himself, noticed their valuables were vanishing and began to suspect it was the work of Catrin, especially as a prized Haggarty family heirloom had been stolen and the only other person besides Pádraic who knew where it was kept was Catrin. Eventually, the new fifth-year Hogwarts student looked into the matter and discovered it wasn't a person at all but a niffler that'd taken everyone's valuables.
