## Biography（英文原文）

Claire and her brother, Bardolph, grew up in Upper Hogsfield. However, in the late 19th century, as Ranrok's Loyalists grew bolder, they began to threaten and attack the people in the area, including Bardolph when he refused to hand over a day's worth of scavenged herbs for his and his sister's shop. After he had recovered, he informed Claire he was going out to seek Victor Rookwood's advice. Claire didn't approve of this, feeling her brother may come to regret the actions he took to protect them, but her brother headed out anyways.
