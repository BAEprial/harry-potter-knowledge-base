## Biography（英文原文）

For a time, Lawang was a traveling merchant and, while arduous, she found it enjoyable to travel the world. At one point her travels took her to Keenbridge and, while she continued to travel and sell, couldn't get her mind off how well suited she found the hamlet. She eventually returned and settled down there, opening a potions shop. Despite her more humble shop and having to buy from Parry Pippin to meet her customers' demands, even Pippin notes Lawang is highly skilled at potions, even though she annoys him with her debates over the quality of his potions.
