# Leander Prewett·遗

> 英文名：Leander Prewett


## 简介（英文原文）

Leander Prewett is a fifth-year Gryffindor student at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. He is a member of the Crossed Wands duelling club and a Summoner's Court competitor.

## Personality（英文原文）

Leander is known to bully Zenobia Noke as he, along with two others, hid her Gobstones after they lost a round to her.

## Involvement（英文原文）

Leander attends Charms, Defence Against the Dark Arts and Herbology Class. Leander can participate in a game of Summoner's Court with the new fifth-year student, and is an opponent in the third round of the Crossed Wands duelling club tournament.

## Associated quests（英文原文）

* Defence Against the Dark Arts Class * Herbology Class * Crossed Wands: Round 3 * Summoner's Court: Match 1 * Astronomy Class * Beasts Class * Potions Class * Professor Weasley's Assignment

## Notes（英文原文）

* If playing as a Gryffindor student, Leander will be the one to announce that Professor Weasley is waiting for you outside the common room on the first day of Hogwarts.
