## Biography（英文原文）

Dumfrey died sometime prior to 1890, where he was beheaded in battle during an early and nearly forgotten goblin rebellion and came back as as ghost. However, he was rather slow to pick up on things and didn't realize he was dead until the battle was long over. By 1890, he had become a member of the Headless Hunt and in 1890, he participated in a Headless Hunt party at the Hogsmeade Graveyard.
