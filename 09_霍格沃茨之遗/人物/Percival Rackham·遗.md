## Biography（英文原文）

=== Early years === Rackham started to attend Hogwarts during his fifth year, where was sorted into Hufflepuff house. At one point he became aware of his ability to see and use Ancient Magic and began to hone this ability. === Adult years === As an adult, he later became a professor at Hogwarts under Headmistress Niamh Fitzgerald. As his ancient magic abilities grew, he began to realize the potential dangers if such knowledge fell into the wrong hands and thus formed the Keepers with Fitzgerald and their colleagues, Charles Rookwood and San Bakar, with the purpose of safeguarding ancient magic. At one point the group used their abilities to revive a drought ridden hamlet, Feldcroft.
