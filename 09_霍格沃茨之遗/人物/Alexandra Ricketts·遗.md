## Biography（英文原文）

Hearing of Ranrok's Loyalists increasing their attacks in the area, Alexandra decided to try and help secure Brocburrow by training a troll to defend them. This didn't go unnoticed, with many of the other residents thinking it was crazy of her to try and do so, even more so when the troll almost trampled half the hamlet. Alexandra ultimately realized her attempts to even teach the troll some words, nevertheless protect the hamlet, was a lost cause, but was hesitant to kill the troll, especially as he was far stronger than any she'd encountered.
