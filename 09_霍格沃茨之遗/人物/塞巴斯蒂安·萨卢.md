## Description（英文原文）

:Sebastian is a charming and extroverted boy whose twin sister, Anne, suffers from a mysterious, debilitating curse. Sebastian is convinced that her last hope for a cure lies in the Dark Arts - and he is determined to find it.

## Biography（英文原文）

Sebastian, along with his twin sister Anne, were born to two professors who instilled in their children a thirst for knowledge. However, while Sebastian and Anne were still young children, they heard a crash from the cellar room where their parents often went to read over books. They ran down to investigate, only to find their parents dead from a defective lamp that caused the room to fill with an undetectable toxin. Their uncle, Solomon, then took the twins into his home at Feldcroft. Sebastian shows such high levels of anger towards his uncle that he ends up killing him. This is because Solomon believes there is no cure for Anne. === Hogwarts === When they were eleven, Sebastian and Anne started to attend Hogwarts, where both were sorted into Slytherin, and they soon became close friends with their fellow Slytherin, Ominis Gaunt.
