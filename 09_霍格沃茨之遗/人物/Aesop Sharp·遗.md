## Description（英文原文）

:The gruff and keenly astute Professor Sharp enjoyed a lengthy and successful career as an Auror until injury forced him from the field. Not wanting to push parchment around a desk at the Ministry, Sharp decided his time would be better spent sharing his extensive experience involving battle-worthy draughts and elixirs with the students at Hogwarts. Not easily impressed and wholly unwilling to suffer fools, he takes genuine pride in students who excel in his class despite his challenging curriculum.

## Biography（英文原文）

Sharp was a successful Auror until he got overconfident and made a severe miscalculation when trying to take down a smuggler in Scarborough, resulting in his partner's death while Sharp barely escaped with his own life. However, this didn't come without consequences, with Sharp having been seriously injured enough that he could no longer do fieldwork. Thus, he decided to teach potions at Hogwarts instead of being confined to a desk job at the Ministry. He also saw this as a calculated move: the Hogwarts library held a wealth of knowledge, which he hoped he could use to find a way to heal his leg.
