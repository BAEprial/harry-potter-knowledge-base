## Biography（英文原文）

Arthur is a half-blood, having grown up with a Muggle mother and Wizard father. His mother in particular did not care for having pets, particularly magical ones, after Arthur's father brought home a puffskein that promptly stuck its tongue up her nose. Thus, Arthur never really saw any magical creatures outside of books until he headed off to Hogwarts, where he was sorted into Hufflepuff.
