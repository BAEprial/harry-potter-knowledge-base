## Biography（英文原文）

Clementine attended Hogwarts where, as a young first year, she and the rest of her classmates were told to stay away from The Forbidden Forest, with horror stories like students getting their heads chopped off to warn them of the dangers. This left such an impression on Clementine that even later as an adult, she refused to go in, despite seeing some beautiful butterflies she wanted to follow.
