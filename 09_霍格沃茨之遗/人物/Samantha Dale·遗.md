## Description（英文原文）

:Samantha Dale is a descendant of Marmaduke Gilbertus Dale, a famed herbologist. She is obsessed with her ancestors and researches stories from her family history. She has an impulsive, generally disbelieving brother named William, who sets out to prove her wrong whenever he can and ends up with cursed legs that have turned into vegetables. Perhaps you can help her reverse the curse?

## Biography（英文原文）

In 1886, Samantha started attending Hogwarts, where she was sorted into Ravenclaw.

## Involvement（英文原文）

Samantha attends Charms and Potions Class. She is a Summoner's Court competitor. During her fifth year, she researches an ancestor of hers, Marmaduke Dale, and learns that his tomb has been cursed due to a crest that was stolen by Marmaduke's older brother out of spite. However, when she relays this information to her own brother, William, he laughs in her face and makes fun of this discovery. He then goes right up to the entrance of the Dale Family Tomb as if to make a point but instead finds his feet had transformed into beets. Samantha then begins to worry that if the curse is not lifted soon, this transformation might be permanent, so she reaches out to the new fifth-year student as the curse only affects those from the Dale family line, and thus the student can take the crest and place it back where it belongs on Marmaduke's sarcophagus to lift the curse.
