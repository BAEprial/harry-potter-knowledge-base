## Biography（英文原文）

Eddie grew up with his sister Helen in Brocburrow. When he later started to attend Hogwarts, he met and befriended Bernard Ndiaye, with the two remaining friends and business associates ever since. After graduating, Eddie returned to Brocburrow and ran a small shop there while his sister went off to London to work for the Ministry as an Auror, though the two still kept in touch. In 1890, he learned Bernard was having problems with some of Ranrok's Loyalists in Feldcroft. Eddie in turn sent a couple crates of Chinese Chomping Cabbages to Bernard to assist him, but the crates got intercepted.
