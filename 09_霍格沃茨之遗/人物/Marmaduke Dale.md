## Biography（英文原文）

Marmaduke was a famed herbologist, having discovered numerous types of flora and properties of several magical herbs and plants, influencing both herbology and potion-making. However, this caused resentment from his older brother, Granum, for all the attention Marmaduke got. This resentment only grew when, after their mother died, she gave the prized family crest to Marmaduke, something Granum felt should have been rightfully his as the eldest. Years later, after Marmaduke had also passed away, Granum stole the crest in retaliation and cursed the tomb so no Dale family member could ever enter it to pay their respects.
