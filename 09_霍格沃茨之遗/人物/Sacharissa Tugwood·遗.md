## Biography（英文原文）

Sacharissa attended Hogwarts where she was sorted into Hufflepuff. Around 1890, while still a student, she was already focusing on magical beauty products, believing they were the future. As part of her research into this business, she wished to find a way to help her fellow students remove unsightly acne from their faces, though this seemed more so she did not have to see the blemishes herself rather than out of altruism on her part. Despite this mindset, she was able to deduce that if bubotuber pus could cause boils, then an altered form could reverse them, and learned her theory was correct on this front. However, she needed more of the plant but did not really want to go through the trouble of gathering them herself, and thus asked the new fifth-year student if they could do so if she paid them.
