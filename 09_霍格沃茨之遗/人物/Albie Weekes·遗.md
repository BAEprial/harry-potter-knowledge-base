## Biography（英文原文）

Albie took a liking to brooms and flying techniques to the point it annoyed his school's flying instructor, Chiyo Kogawa, as she preferred he'd focus on the art of flying. That said, they both saw it fitting that after he graduated from Hogwarts, he began to run his own shop selling brooms as Albie loved the more technical side rather than join a Quidditch team. While he sold brooms, he also spent his free time trying to find ways to improve them, though others felt this was a lost cause, viewing brooms as something that couldn't be improved. He originally tried to enlist Imelda Reyes to help gather the data he needed for broom improvements, but when he pointed out some flaws in her flying techniques, she refused to assist him further.
