## Biography（英文原文）

Archie lived with his parents in Lower Hogsfield when, in 1890, his father had become too much of an annoyance for the Rookwood Gang's second-in-command, Theophilus Harlow, to ignore anymore. Harlow paid a visit to the Bickle's home while Johanna was away and summarily killed Archie's father right in front of him. Archie took off into the wilderness but was soon caught by other members of the Rookwood Gang and held prisoner while they waited for Harlow's orders on what to do with him. While being held prisoner, Archie overheard the criminals talk about how Harlow was going to go after several of his parents' friends, including Isko and Daisy Rabe, Agabus Philbert, and Otto Dibble. Thankfully, Archie was soon rescued by Natsai Onai and a new fifth-year student and returned safely to his mother, where he informed her the others were in danger.
