## 简介（英文原文）

Ominis Gaunt is a fifth-year Slytherin student at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry and a descendant of Salazar Slytherin. His family enjoyed using the Cruciatus Curse on innocent Muggles, and this made him grow to hate the Dark Arts, one of his most notable traits.

## Biography（英文原文）

Ominis was born blind and no magic was able to cure it, but he learned to work around this by using his wand to help him navigate. He also had a very contentious relationship with most of his family, whose pure-blood views he opposed and which only his aunt, Noctua, shared the same sentiments as Ominis, making her by far his favorite relative. Ominis' resentment only grew more when most of his family, who loved to cast Crucio on Muggles for sport, tried to get him to do it as a child, but he refused when he heard the horrifying screams. His family then cast it on him instead, causing him a lot of pain. Not wishing to face the pain again, when he was pressed to cast it once more, he finally relented, though he continued to regret this years later. Despite opposing the rest of his family, his family did impart on him secrets about Hogwarts, including a room nobody else seemed to be aware of that he took to calling the Undercroft. He also became close friends with his fellow Slytherin, Sebastian Sallow, and Sebastian's twin sister, Anne, and showed them the Undercroft. The three would regularly hang out here together, practicing spells typically not taught to students and playing gobs
