## Description（英文原文）

:Amit aspires to be a famous wizarding historian and already has extensive plans for his first memoir. Exceptionally bright and well-read, he adores stargazing and always seems to have the most recent top-of-the-line telescope at hand. Amit will leap at the chance to help a friend in need, but once in the thick of things often realises he might be better off reading or writing about certain adventures than experiencing them.

## Biography（英文原文）

From an early age, Amit took every chance he could get to learn new things, one of which was to teach himself Gobbledegook thanks to his parents having an extensive library. However, being self-taught without having ever met an actual goblin meant he could only read it but not speak it. He also took a particular interest in stargazing, going out of his way to buy the best equipment he could get a hold of to further his studies.
