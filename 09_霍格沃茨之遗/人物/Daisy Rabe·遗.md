## Biography（英文原文）

In the late 19th century, she and her husband, Isko, both worked at Gringotts, and were friends with the Bickles. Due to her position at the bank, she had access to the vaults on a regular basis and Theophilus Harlow approached her in the hopes she'd help him extort her colleagues so he could access some of the treasures within. However, Daisy firmly declined and thought that was the end of it until she came home one day to find her husband's prized flowers in the garden trampled, Isko missing, and a note left behind, clearly insinuating if she didn't help Harlow, they'd harm her husband. Even if she wished to help though, Daisy knew Isko would never want her to compromise on her integrity, even for his sake, but was stuck at an impasse.
