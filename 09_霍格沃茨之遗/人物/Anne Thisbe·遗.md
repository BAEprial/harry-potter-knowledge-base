Anne Thisbe was a Muggle-born witch and former Hogwarts student in .

## Biography（英文原文）

Anne attended Hogwarts around the same time as Richard Jackdaw and Apollonia Black. Richard had initially shown interest in the latter, but when it was made apparent Apollonia had no interest in Richard, he moved on to other pursuits. Eventually, Anne and Richard became romantically involved and at one point, he promised Anne an adventure but only after she solved a series of puzzles he set up. Unfortunately for Anne, she wasn't able to solve the puzzle and so never got the clue he left behind on where to meet up with him in the Forbidden Forest. A short time later, Richard's decapitated ghost showed up in Hogsmeade but just as quickly left the area, leaving everyone wondering who had killed him. Apollonia took advantage of the situation as, while she had no interest in Richard she was nonetheless jealous he went after someone else, and falsely accused Anne under the flimsiest of pretexts that Anne threatened to kill Richard for breaking her heart. Combined with how powerful the Black family's power was over certain people in the Ministry and the fact that Anne was a Muggle-born all but sealed her fate and she was sentenced to Azkaban.
