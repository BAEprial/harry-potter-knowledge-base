Stupefy is an Essential spell in . It was taught to the new student by Professor Fig prior to the start of the game.

## Description（英文原文）

Stuns enemies, making them easy targets for follow-up spells. It deals no direct damage but stunned enemies take extra damage, indicated by gold numbers. It also breaks enemy Shield Charms (except on Hard difficulty). When successfully deflecting an incoming attack with Protego, keep Q held to cast a Stupefy counter-attack at whichever enemy you choose to target.

## Uses（英文原文）

Stupefy is used as a counter-attack, following a successful block with Protego. Stunned enemies will be unable to attack for some time, leaving them open to a counter attack. While Stupefy usually does not deal damage, it can be upgraded to do so. On lower difficulty levels (Story, Easy, or Normal), Stupefy will also break enemy Shield Charms, regardless of type.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

Stupefy has the following upgrades: === Stupefy Mastery === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Core Section. Enemies stunned by Stupefy will remain stunned for a longer period of time, granting more opportunities for counter attacking. === Stupefy Expertise === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Core Section. In addition to stunning an enemy, Stupefy will deal direct damage on impact. === Stunning Curse === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Dark Arts Section. Stupefy will curse enemies on impact, causing them to take increased damage.

## Trivia（英文原文）

* Stupefy comes from the Latin word "stupere", which means "to strike senseless" - alluding to its stunning effect. * Stupefy is the only spell in Hogwarts Legacy which cannot be cast on its own. It is always cast as a follow-up to Protego, as the button input for both spells is the same.
