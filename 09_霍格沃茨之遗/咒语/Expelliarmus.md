# Expelliarmus

> 英文名：Expelliarmus


## 简介（英文原文）

Expelliarmus is a Damage spell in . It is learned from Professor Dinah Hecat, during her second assignment.

## 基本信息

| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 名称 | Expelliarmus |
| 类型 | Damage |
| 描述 | Disarm and damage an enemy |
| 解锁条件 | Prof. Hecat's Assignment 2 |

## Description（英文原文）

Disarms wands and weapons from most enemies who wield them. Also deals damage to all enemies, even if they do not carry a weapon.

## Uses（英文原文）

Expelliarmus deals direct damage. If the target is holding an object, they will lose their grip on it. Expelliarmus can be used to disarm enemy wizards of their wands, or goblins and Pensieve Guardians of their weapons. Disarmed enemies will panic for a brief moment, before rushing to retrieve their dropped items to get back in the fight. While disarmed, enemies are open to attack, allowing players to unleash combos on them before they can respond in kind. Expelliarmus can also be used with the Ancient Magic Throw, to hurl enemy weapons as projectiles.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

Expelliarmus can be upgraded as follows: === Disarming Curse === Enemies hit by Expelliarmus will become Cursed, causing them to take extra damage from attacks. Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Dark Arts Section. === Ancient Magic Throw Expertise === After disarming a target with Expelliarmus, the player can use the Ancient Magic Throw on the disarmed weapon to throw it at an enemy. Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Core Section.

## Trivia（英文原文）

* In the novels and films, Expelliarmus is Harry Potter's signature spell, due to his frequent usage of it in battle.
