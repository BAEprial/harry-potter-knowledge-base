# Transformation

> 英文名：Transformation


## 简介（英文原文）

Transformation is a Control spell in . It is learned from Professor Weasley during her Assignment. It temporarily transforms objects and enemies into random objects, and breaks yellow Shield Charms. If cast on an Animagus while in their animal form, they are forced to revert to human form. A transformed enemy can be hurled at other enemies by using Ancient Magic Throw.
