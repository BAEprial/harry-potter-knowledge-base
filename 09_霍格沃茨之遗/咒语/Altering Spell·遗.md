# Altering Spell·遗

> 英文名：Altering Spell


## 简介（英文原文）

The Altering Spell is a Transfiguration spell in . It is used to change the physical form or colour of conjured objects in the Room of Requirement, and cannot be used outside the room. The spell is learned from Professor Weasley during the side quest Interior Decorating.
