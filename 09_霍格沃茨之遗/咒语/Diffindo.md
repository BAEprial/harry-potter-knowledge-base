## Capabilities（英文原文）

Diffindo is a long-range attack, which creates a damaging blade made from magic energy. As a damage spell, Diffindo can break red Shield Charms. The energy blade formed through the spell has a wide hitbox, allowing it to hit multiple enemies in close proximity. Diffindo can also be upgraded to hit multiple enemies standing in line. Diffindo can also be used to cut through ropes holding up objects out of reach, and release hanging bodies in Spider Dens.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

Diffindo can be upgraded using Talent Points. === Diffindo Mastery === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Core Section. Diffindo casts will pass through enemies, allowing the caster to strike multiple targets in a line.
