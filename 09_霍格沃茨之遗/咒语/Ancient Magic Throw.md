# Ancient Magic Throw

> 英文名：Ancient Magic Throw


## 简介（英文原文）

Ancient Magic Throw is an Essential spell in . It is used to pick up miscellaneous objects and throw them at an enemy, and can break Shield Charms regardless of colour. It is unlocked during Welcome to Hogsmeade. The spell can only be used on certain environmental objects, which are marked by the spell's key/button prompt.
