## Uses（英文原文）

Revelio highlights items that can be interacted with, such as magical items, loot chests and puzzle components. These will glow blue in the player's field of vision. Revelio can also be used to reveal invisible Field Guide pages distributed throughout the map. When staking out enemy strongholds, or in the overworld, Revelio can also reveal their relative positions to the player. Enemies highlighted with Revelio will glow red in the player's field of vision. Revelio can also be used while mounted on a beast or broomstick, allowing the player to scan parts of the game world while travelling from place to place.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

Revelio has the following upgrades: === Revelio Mastery === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Core Section. This increases the effective range of Revelio, allowing the player to scan for items in a wider range.

## Trivia（英文原文）

* Revelio translates to "I reveal" in Latin. * Revelio is the first spell the player learns in the game.
