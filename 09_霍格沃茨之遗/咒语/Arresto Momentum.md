# Arresto Momentum

> 英文名：Arresto Momentum


## 简介（英文原文）

Arresto Momentum is a Control spell in . It is learned from Madam Chiyo Kogawa during her second assignment.

## Description（英文原文）

Slows both objects and enemies, giving you extra time to plan your next move.

## Uses（英文原文）

Arresto Momentum will slow targets that are in motion. The duration which the slow lasts for depends on the size of the target - larger targets will be slowed for a shorter duration, and vice-versa. As a Control spell, it can break yellow Shield Charms. The spell can be used on charging enemies, slowing them down and granting the player more time to dodge or prepare a counterattack. If used on airborne enemies, they will be suspended in midair, allowing the player to attack or shift focus to another enemy.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

Arresto Momentum has the following upgrades: === Slowing Curse === Unlocked through spending Talent Points in the Dark Arts section. An enemy hit with Arresto Momentum will be cursed, causing them to take more damage from other attacks.
