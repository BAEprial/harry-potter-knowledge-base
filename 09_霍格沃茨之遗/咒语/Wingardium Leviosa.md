# Wingardium Leviosa

> 英文名：Wingardium Leviosa

> 正典关联：[[羽加迪姆·勒维奥萨]]


## 简介（英文原文）

Wingardium Leviosa is a Utility spell available in . It is an advanced form of Levioso, and is used to levitate and move objects. It is learned from Professor Garlick during her Assignment 1. Accio also gains Wingardium Leviosa as a secondary effect once you get this spell, however it cannot move the large stone spheres in some Merlin Trials whereas the standalone spell can.
