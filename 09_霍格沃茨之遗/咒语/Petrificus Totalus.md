> 英文名：Petrificus Totalus

> 正典关联：[[统统石化]]


## 简介（英文原文）

Petrificus Totalus, also called the Full Body-Bind Curse, is an Essential spell in . It petrifies most enemies, defeating them instantly. More powerful enemies are affected only briefly, taking some damage. It is unlocked upon completion of Secrets of the Restricted Section.
