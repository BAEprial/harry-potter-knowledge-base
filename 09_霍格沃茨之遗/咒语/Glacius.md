# Glacius

> 英文名：Glacius

## Description（英文原文）

Freezes enemies, increasing the damage they take from follow-up attacks.

## Uses（英文原文）

Glacius freezes enemies in their tracks, rendering them unable to move, attack, or defend themselves. Frozen enemies will fall to the ground and will remain inactive for the duration of the spell. They will also take increased damage from subsequent spells against them. As a Control spell, Glacius is effective against yellow shield charms. Glacius can also freeze moving objects in place, which is necessary for solving some puzzles within the game world. While Glacius does not deal direct damage on its own, it is a useful spell for augmenting the power of other combat spells. Use it to increase enemies' vulnerability to your attacks, or reduce the number of enemies actively targeting you. Glacius is less effective at freezing larger enemies, such as trolls and the larger spider enemies.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

Glacius has the following upgrades: === Glacius Mastery === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Spells Section. Hitting an enemy frozen by Glacius will cause them to emit shards of ice. These shards will damage enemies within their area of effect.

## Trivia（英文原文）

* Glacius is the Latin word for 'ice'. * Glacius is the only spell in Hogwarts Legacy that has never been seen cast in any of the Wizarding World films.
