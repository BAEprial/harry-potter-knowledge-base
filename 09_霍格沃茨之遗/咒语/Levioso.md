# Levioso

> 英文名：Levioso


## 简介（英文原文）

Levioso is a Control spell in . It levitates an object or enemy. The spell is learned from Professor Hecat during Defence Against the Dark Arts Class.

## Description（英文原文）

Levitates objects and enemies. Useful for solving puzzles and surprising enemies alike.

## Uses（英文原文）

In combat, Levioso can be used to levitate some enemies, leaving them unable to attack or defend themselves. The spell can also be used to break yellow Shield Charms. Levioso is also required for some puzzles, to levitate or activate objects that bear a Levioso symbol.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

=== Levioso Mastery === Enemies near a target levitated by Levioso are also levitated.
