# Ancient Magic (Spell)

> 英文名：Ancient Magic (Spell)


## 简介（英文原文）

Ancient Magic is an Essential spell in . It deals a massive amount of damage to an enemy, but can only be used when at least one segment of the Ancient Magic Meter is full. It is unlocked during the main quest Welcome to Hogsmeade, in which it is used to deliver the finishing blow to an Armoured Troll.
