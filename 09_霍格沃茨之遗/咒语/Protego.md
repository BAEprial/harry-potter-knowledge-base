## Description（英文原文）

Protects against a variety of attacks, including spell casts, weapon strikes, and more. Cast with Q. Waiting to cast Protego until the last moment before an impact results in a Perfect Protego, that damages melee attackers and reflects projectiles back, breaking enemy shields.

## Uses（英文原文）

Protego is an essential tool in protecting oneself from enemy attacks. Use it to counter most melee and ranged attacks from enemies, human or beast. Casting it just before an attack impacts a player results in a Perfect Protego, which not only damages enemies, but knocks them back and opens up space to move or counter-attack. A Perfect Protego is also effective against ranged magic attacks, and will reflect the projectile back at its shooter. When playing on lower difficulties, a yellow circle will appear on top of the player's head when an attack is imminent. Attacks with these yellow circles can be blocked by Protego. Protego is ineffective against attacks with a red circle (which must be dodged). These include some damaging spells used by enemies, and heavy melee attacks such as those from trolls and larger spider enemies.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

Protego has the following upgrades: === Protego Absorption === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Core Section. Successful blocks with Protego will contribute to the Ancient Magic meter, with Perfect Protego blocks contributing even more. === Protego Expertise === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Core Section. Performing a Perfect Protego to block a projectile attack from an enemy will send back two projectiles instead of one. === Protego Mastery === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Core Section. Performing a Perfect Protego will release a damaging blast that breaks enemy shield charms nearby, regardless of colour.
