## Description（英文原文）

A long-range bolt that deals damage on impact. Enemies hit with fire-based attacks will continue to take damage for a few seconds, during which time collisions will result in incendiary bursts.

## Uses（英文原文）

Confringo sends out bolts of fire which damage enemies. It is similar to Incendio, but it has a longer range. As a damage spell, Confringo is effective at breaking red Shield Charms. Confringo can be used in combinations as a go-to damage spell, and is necessary when fighting against Inferi. It can also be used as an alternative to Incendio to burn through spiderwebs, and to light torches and braziers to solve certain puzzles, as some may be out of the former's effective range.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

Confringo has the following upgrades: === Confringo Mastery === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Spells Section. Successful impacts with Confringo will create additional fiery bolts, which will seek other enemies in the same vicinity.
