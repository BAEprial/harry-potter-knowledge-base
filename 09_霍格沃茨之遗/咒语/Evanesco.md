# Evanesco

> 英文名：Evanesco


## 简介（英文原文）

Evanesco is a Transfiguration spell in . It is used to destroy conjured objects, converting them back into Moonstone. The spell can only be used in the Room of Requirement, and is learned from Professor Weasley during the main quest The Room of Requirement.

## Trivia（英文原文）

* Evanesco is one of the few interchangeable spells in Hogwarts Legacy that do not have any Talent Upgrades, the others being Wingardium Leviosa, The Conjuring Spell, the Altering Spell, Lumos, and Reparo.
