# Flipendo

> 英文名：Flipendo


## 简介（英文原文）

Flipendo is a Force spell in . It is learned from Professor Mirabel Garlick during her second assignment.

## Description（英文原文）

Flips objects and enemies upwards and backwards. With its short cooldown, it is excellent for setting up and extending juggles. Its ability to flip objects instantly is useful for solving puzzles and exploiting certain enemies' weaknesses.

## Uses（英文原文）

Known as the Knockback Jinx, Flipendo will flip a target head over heels backwards, leaving them in the air for a few seconds. While in midair, enemies are open to being attacked by other damaging spells and combinations. As a force spell, Flipendo will break purple Shield Charms. Flipendo is effective as a combination spell against Dugbogs while they recover from their charge attack, and against burrowing spider enemies. Ripping them from the ground will deal massive damage to them. Flipendo is especially useful when fighting Trolls. After a Troll slams its club into the ground, they become vulnerable to having it flipped up back into their faces with the Knockback Jinx, leaving them open to more attacks.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

Flipendo has the following upgrades: === Knockback Curse === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Dark Arts Section. Hitting a target with Flipendo will curse them, causing them to take extra damage from subsequent attacks.

## Trivia（英文原文）

* At just five seconds, Flipendo has the shortest cooldown of all the combat-oriented spells.
