> 英文名：Conjuring Spell


## 简介（英文原文）

The Conjuring Spell is a Transfiguration spell in . It is used to conjure objects in the Room of Requirement, and cannot be used outside the room. The spell is learned from Professor Weasley during the main quest The Room of Requirement.
