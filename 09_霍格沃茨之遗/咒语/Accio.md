# Accio

> 英文名：Accio


## 简介（英文原文）

Accio is a force spell in . It is learned from Professor Abraham Ronen during Charms Class.

## Description（英文原文）

Summons a variety of objects and enemies to close range. Certain magical and heavy objects require sustained effort to pull close. When a summoned object reaches you, you will automatically cast Wingardium Leviosa to continue levitating and controlling it without the need for additional button inputs.

## Uses（英文原文）

Accio is one of the first spells learned in the game. In combat, players can use the Summoning Charm to draw enemies closer to them, allowing for easier targeting when unleashing combos. Accio is particularly effective against Rangers, since they tend to stay on the fringes of battle and are poor at close combat. As a Force Spell, Accio will also break purple Shield Charms used by some enemies. Accio may be used to pull the player to objects, as seen in some of the puzzle rooms found in Hogwarts Castle. Accio can also be used to summon objects to the player, such as flying Field Guide Pages, hard-to-reach Gobstones, and environmental objects. In the game of Summoner's Courtt, Accio is used to summon the balls towards the player into the different scoring zones. When an object summoned with Accio reaches the player, they will automatically cast Wingardium Leviosa to continue levitating and controlling it, without the need for additional button inputs. Accio is ineffective against large enemies, such as Trolls, larger Pensieve enemies, and larger spiders.

## Upgrades（英文原文）

Accio has the following upgrades: === Accio Mastery === Unlocked by spending Talent Points in the Spells Section. This increases the area of effect of Accio. Any enemies that are within the vicinity of a target summoned by Accio will also be summoned.
