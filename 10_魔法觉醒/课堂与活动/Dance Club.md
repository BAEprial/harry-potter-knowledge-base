## Gameplay Rules（英文原文）

* Students can attend the Dance Club either by themselves or with a date of their choosing. * With enough Stamina, other player-controlled students in the dance room can also be asked for a dance. * Regain Stamina by purchasing drinks. Stamina allows you to invite other players to dance. * Dances of higher difficulty can be unlocked by reaching their required dance rating. * Dances unlocked by special methods will not count towards the total score rankings. However, single-dance rankings for these dances will still be available. * Receive a random Fireworks each time you attend the Dance Club. [Need to be proof-read] * Every dance has a chance to drop a Madam's Student Coupon or Madam's VIP Coupon. As many as 2 coupons can be obtained each day. * The accuracy of the dance is graded by C, A, S, SS and SSS. * Songs are leveled by stars which shows its difficulty.

## Drinks（英文原文）

All drinks required gems to buy.

## Partners（英文原文）

Ivy, Daniel, Lottie, Abigail, Cassandra. However, Cassandra requires a 5 star difficulty in a song to be unlocked.
