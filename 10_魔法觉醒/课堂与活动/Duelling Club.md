# Duelling Club

> 英文名：Duelling Club


## 简介（英文原文）

}}The Duelling Club is an activity hub for Duelling either alone or with a partner. There can be events that have unique rewards for winning duels such as the Chocolate Frog Sweets Spree.

## Duelling Club Rules（英文原文）

===Solo Duel === * In Solo Duel, you'll be randomly matched against another opponent based on your Tier. * MP Regen Speed increases 90s into a Duel. * 3 minutes into a Duel, both sides will lose an increasing percentage of Health every 5 seconds. * You will be subject to a weekly Card ban once you reach Expert Tier. === Duo Duel === * In Duo Duel, or Best Partners, you'll be randomly paired with another student, and will be randomly matched against two students based on your Tier. * MP Regen Speed increases 120s into a Duel. * 4 minutes into a Duel, both sides will lose an increasing percentage of Health every 5 seconds. *When a teammate is defeated, you can tap the icon above them to begin rescuing. A successful rescue will restore a small amount of Health to the teammate. * You will be subject to a weekly Card ban once you reach Expert Tier.

## Tiers and Ranking（英文原文）

===Tier Up=== *Exceeding the upper limit of the Tier Points for your current Tier Grade will elevate it by one Grade. *Reaching the highest Grade for your current Tier and exceeding the upper limit of the Tier Points for that Grade will promote you to the next Tier. *Tier rewards will be determined by your highest Tier in all Duelling Modes. Rewards for each Tier can be claimed only once. *When your Tier in one mode vastly exceeds those for other modes, your Tiers in the other modes will be raised by a certain percentage after the daily 5:00 AM Tier Update. *If a duel is underway during the cut-off time for the Tier Update, Tier Points awarded for that match will be implemented after any Tier changes have taken effect in the update. *Win streaks and defeating powerful opponents grant more Tier Points. The Point bonus slowly decreases as your Tier increases, eventually reaching 0. ===Ranking=== *Students are ranked according to their Tier Grades and Tier Points. *For students with the same Tier Points, the first comer will be ranked higher.

## Season（英文原文）

* Ranking will be reset with the end of each season, on which day Ranked Matches will be unavailable from 5:00 AM to 5:30 AM. Tier Grades and Points higher than a certain scope will be downgraded and cleared, while those within the scope will remain unchanged. * The results of any battles begun right before the reset will still be counted.
