# 禁林·觉醒

> 英文名：Forbidden Forest


## 简介（英文原文）

The Forbidden Forest is a roguelike game mode that allows players to battle various enemies for rewards. It is the primary method for collecting Echoes and features an idle collection mechanic that accumulates rewards over time.

## How It Works（英文原文）

When exploring in the Forbidden Forest, players will be able to select different paths (events) for each stage. Complete all stages to reach the final boss, defeating which will mark the end of the exploration. * Select an event to experience for each round of forest exploration. * Carefully consider which event to pick based on your current status. * Challenge and beat the final boss to complete the current exploration. === Event List === Different events require different actions from the player, and will provide different rewards. * Battle * Battle (Hard) * Forest Merchant * Crisis * Gathering Spot * Free Exploration * Resting Spot

## Echo Conversion（英文原文）

Echo Conversion is a mechanic that allows players to convert Echoes of certain rarities into Echo Crystals. This applies to all Echoes obtained in the Forbidden Forest, such as those from Into the Woods (Echo), not just those collected by the Flying Ford Anglia. Players can choose to convert individual Echoes at separate rates or choose the same setting for all Echoes. The available settings allow players to convert either Common Echoes, Rare Echoes and below, Epic Echoes and below, or do no conversion at all. All conversions are final. Echoes cannot be recovered after being converted into Crystals.
