# Derivative Card

> 英文名：Derivative Card


## 简介（英文原文）

Derivative Cards are Cards that only exist through certain Card effects. These will not be returned to your Deck once used.

## Cards（英文原文）

Cards that qualify as Derivative Cards are: * Movement Cards * Apparition * Immobulus * Thestral * Wild Boar * Empty Head * Wingardium Leviosa * Sword Of Gryffindor * Patronus Charm (Book with No Name Edtion) * Red Obscurus[Unoffical Name] * Qui's beam[Unoffical Name] * Qui's Whip[Unoffical Name] * Diffusion Whip[Unoffical Name] *
