# 卢娜·洛夫古德·卡牌

> 英文名：Luna Lovegood (Card)


## 简介（英文原文）

Luna Lovegood is a Legendary Companion. This Card is one of that have been added to Harry Potter: Magic Awakened, as well as one of Companions currently available. ==Details== ﻿Summons Luna Lovegood. When you use a Spell Card, Luna will cast a random Summon that costs 1 MP less than the Spell Card played. The Summon shares the same Card Level as Luna.

## Details（英文原文）

﻿Summons Luna Lovegood. When you use a Spell Card, Luna will cast a random Summon that costs 1 MP less than the Spell Card played. The Summon shares the same Card Level as Luna. After spending 20 MP on Spell Cards, Luna will call forth a Thestral to charge at the target location, dealing AoE Damage before striking 6 times in quick succession. If 5 or more strikes successfully hit your opponents, or if the Thestral defeats an opponent while charging, it will split into 2 and charge at the closest opponent, each dealing 50% of the original Damage.
