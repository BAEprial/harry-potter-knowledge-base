# 邓布利多·卡牌

> 正典关联：[[阿不思·邓布利多]]


## 简介（英文原文）

}}Albus Dumbledore is a Mythic Companion. This Card is one of that have been added to Harry Potter: Magic Awakened, as well as one of Companions currently available. ==Details== ﻿Summons Albus Dumbledore, who increases friendly players' Magic cast within a set range surrounding him, and marks these Cards: Unforgivable Curses, Dark Spells, and their derivative Spells. *When marked Cards are drawn and used again, they earn the following benefits: Spell Cards are cast 1 more time but do less Damage. Summons revive 1 time after being defeated, but with reduced Health. *Spell and Summon Cards that are enhanced share the same Card Level as Albus Dumbledore. *Each time he takes Damage, Dumbledore will apparate to a random location. 15 seconds after being summoned, Dumbledore will leave of his own accord. ==Gallery==
