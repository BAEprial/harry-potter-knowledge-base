# 哈利·波特·卡牌

> 英文名：Harry Potter (Card)


## 简介（英文原文）

*Front-Row (✦✦✦✦✧)|category=Companion|rarity=Legendary|obtain=*Magical Studies (Global)}}Harry Potter is a Legendary Companion. This Card is one of that have been added to Harry Potter: Magic Awakened, as well as one of Companions currently available.

## Details（英文原文）

﻿Summons Harry Potter. Harry will cast Expecto Patronum and continuously knock back opponent units within range at the cost of his Health. * Once per summon, Harry will protect you from any damage that would otherwise defeat you, granting a brief invincibility effect, after which he will retreat from the arena and regenerate a fixed amount of Health.
