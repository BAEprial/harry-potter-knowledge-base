}}Unicorn is a Epic Summon. This Card is one of that have been added to Harry Potter: Magic Awakened, as well as one of Summons currently available.

## Details（英文原文）

Summons an injured Unicorn that gradually recovers Health. Once at full Health, it will immediately launch a powerful charge at the farthest enemy.
