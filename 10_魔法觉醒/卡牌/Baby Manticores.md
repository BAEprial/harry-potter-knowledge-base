> 英文名：Baby Manticores


## 简介（英文原文）

}}Baby Manticores is a Epic Summon. This Card is one of that have been added to Harry Potter: Magic Awakened, as well as one of Summons currently available.

## Details（英文原文）

Imitate the movement of a Manticore for 10s, during which your Movement Speed is enhanced and no Movement Cards are spent (Apparition and Hagrid's Motorbike excluded). Control effects can interrupt this process. However, if uninterrupted throughout the duration, you will Encourage allies, increasing their Movement Speed.
