# Young Harry Potter (Card)

> 英文名：Young Harry Potter (Card)


## 简介（英文原文）

}}Young Harry Potter is a Mythic Companion. This Card is one of that have been added to Harry Potter: Magic Awakened, as well as one of Companions currently available. ==Details== ﻿Every time you use two Cards, Harry Potter (Throwback) will apparate and launch an attack. The power of the attack scales with the MP cost difference between the two cards previously used.

## Details（英文原文）

﻿Every time you use two Cards, Harry Potter (Throwback) will apparate and launch an attack. The power of the attack scales with the MP cost difference between the two cards previously used. * 0 or 1 MP Difference: Harry attacks the closest opposing duellist. * 2 or 3 MP Difference: Harry's attack explodes, dealing extra damage and slowing opponents in range. * 4 or 4+ MP Difference: Harry's attack will deal extra damage equal to 10% of the opposing duellist's Health lost.
