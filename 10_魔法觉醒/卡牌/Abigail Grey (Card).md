## 简介（英文原文）

Abigail Grey is an Epic Companion. This Card is one of that have been added to Harry Potter: Magic Awakened, as well as one of Companions currently available. ==Abigail Grey== Summon Abigail to fight alongside you. Placing Abigail close spaws her in Witch form, while placing her away spaws in Werewolf Form.

## Abigail Grey（英文原文）

Summon Abigail to fight alongside you. Placing Abigail close spaws her in Witch form, while placing her away spaws in Werewolf Form. *In Witch Form, Abigail performs basic attacks with her wand and periodically casts Incarcerous (Card) at the same level as her Companion Card to bind opponents. *In Werewolf Form, Abigail spaws next to the player before moving towards the target. When close enough, she pounces at the target location, dealing AoE damage and marking a nearby unit. Marked units temporarily take additional damage.
