## 简介（英文原文）

}}Baby Antipodean Opaleye is a Legendary Summon. This Card is one of that have been added to Harry Potter: Magic Awakened, as well as one of Summons currently available.

Summons a baby Antipodean Opaleye that breathes fire on opponent units, dealing AoE Damage.
