# Young Hermione Granger (Card)

> 英文名：Young Hermione Granger (Card)


## 简介（英文原文）

}}Young Hermione Granger is a Mythic Companion. This Card is one of that have been added to Harry Potter: Magic Awakened, as well as one of Companions currently available. ==Details== ﻿When you summon Young Hermione, the MP Cost of all Cards in your Hand is reduced by 1. Their MP Cost will return to normal after they have been used. Young Hermione will also use Learning Effort to learn a Spell from your opponent, add it to your Deck as a Derivative Card, and reduce its MP Cost by 3. The Spell Level will be the same as that of the Young Hermione Card. Every time Hermione learns, she expends more 'Learning Effort'. The learning process is accelerated when opponents use Spell Cards.
