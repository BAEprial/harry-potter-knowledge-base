# Echo

> 英文名：Echo


## 简介（英文原文）

The Echo(es) are legendary characters "summoned" to assist the player in battle. Every Echo has a unique ability that synergizes differently depending on the Deck it is assigned. Assigning an Echo to your Deck can aid you in combat with its unique ability while also providing a Health and Attack bonus. Echoes can also grant Extra Level Bonuses when the Echo Bonus Card is the same as the current Card. Echoes can be obtained by either the Flying Ford Anglia or doing explorations in The Forbidden Forest.

## Upgrading Echoes（英文原文）

﻿Echoes can be levelled up using Echo Crystals or other Echoes, which increases their Health and Attack Bonus, and unlocks Bonus Card slots. The maximum level of an Echo is determined by its rarity.

## Obtaining Bonus Card Levels（英文原文）

* Echoes can provide a random Level Bonus to Cards in the Deck they're equipped to. * Upgrading an Echo can unlock additional Card Bonuses. The Level Bonuses offered by Echoes vary depending on their rarity, with Epics and higher increasing Spell Levels by up to +2. Note: An Echo's Card Level Bonus will only take effect when it is equipped.

## Echoes（英文原文）

Echoes provide special modifiers to your avatar and deck of cards. Each echo impacts your cards in a different way, they possess different abilities and attributes that can be utilized to create varied combat strategies, so it’s important to build your deck with your echo in mind. They can also be upgraded to enhance their statistics and abilities. For example, the Echo of Harry Potter increases the damage and healing power of low cost spells, making for a highly impactful and fast playing deck should you stack it with low cost cards.
