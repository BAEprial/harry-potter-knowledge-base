# 贝拉特里克斯·莱斯特兰奇·回响

> 英文名：Bellatrix Lestrange (Echo)


## 简介（英文原文）

Every time you damage an opponent, your Fallen Bar will increase by 4 (and 1 if the target is not a fellow student). Filling up the Fallen Bar to 124/138/152 will summon 1 Death Eater. The bar will start to drop until it reaches 0, at which point the accumulation starts again.

## Ability（英文原文）

=== Fallen Bar === Every time you damage an opponent, your Fallen Bar will increase by 4 (and 1 if the target is not a fellow student). Filling up the Fallen Bar to 124/138/152 will summon 1 Death Eater. The bar will start to drop until it reaches 0, at which point the accumulation starts again. === Death Eater === Every time your Fallen Bar drops to 0, an additional Death Eater will be summoned the next time it fills up. As many as 3 Death Eaters can be summoned at the same time. The Death Eaters' attributes will be based on the average level of your Deck and the rarity of your current Echo.
