# The Weasley Twins (Echo)

> 英文名：The Weasley Twins (Echo)


## 简介（英文原文）

The Weasley Twins are Fred and George who are inseparable. They know that the most precious things in the world are family and friends. Echoes of the Weasley Twins can be acquired by completing Whispering Willows in the Forbidden Forest.
