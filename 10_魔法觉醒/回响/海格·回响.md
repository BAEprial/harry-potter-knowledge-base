## Ability（英文原文）

=== Summon Card Enhancement === Attack Speed of your highest-cost Summon +50%/+40%/+30%. Every time this Summon takes total damage equal to 50% of its Max Health, it will recover 28%/26%/24% of its Max Health within 2s. This effect can be triggered up to 3 times. When multiple Summons share the same highest cost within the Deck, one will be randomly chosen.
