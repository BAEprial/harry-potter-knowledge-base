# 多比·回响

> 英文名：Dobby (Echo)

> 正典关联：[[多比]]


## 简介（英文原文）

Grants 3 extra Movement Cards.

## 基本信息

| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 能力 | Movement Cards, Apparition, Phantom Dobby |
| 获取方式 | Whispering Willows |

## Ability（英文原文）

=== Movement Cards === Grants 3 extra Movement Cards. === Apparition === For every Movement Card used, your next Movement Card will be replaced with Apparition. === Phantom Dobby === After using Apparition, a phantom is created at your original position. The opponent that defeats this phantom will take damage equal to 126%/70% of your Attack.
