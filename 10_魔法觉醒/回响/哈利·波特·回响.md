# 哈利·波特·回响

> 英文名：Harry Potter (Echo)


## 简介（英文原文）

As an Auror with ample combat experience, Harry Potter will have no trouble enhancing the effects of the simplest Spells in your Deck. Echoes of Harry Potter can be acquired by completing Midnight Moonshine in the Forbidden Forest.

## 基本信息

| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 能力 | Simple Spells, Enhanced Effects |
| 获取方式 | Midnight Moonshine |

## Ability（英文原文）

=== Simple Spells === Enhances Spell Cards with an MP Cost of 3 or lower. === Enhanced Effects === Enhanced Cards receive a 49%/47%/45%Damage increase and a 17%/15%/13% Healing increase.
