## Details（英文原文）

Using a 2 MP Spell Card will simply cycle through the current Magical Creature Card and offer you a different Summon of the same MP cost. Increasing the level of the Magical Creature Card will allow you to summon creatures of a higher original MP Cost, as follows: * Level 1: 2 MP Cost Summons such as Tebo (Wild Boar) or Niffler * Level 2: 3 MP Cost Summons such as Ashwinder * Level 3: 4 MP Cost Summons such as Matagot * Level 4: 5 MP Cost Summons such as Whomping Willow * Level 5: 6 MP Cost Summons such as Baby Antipodean Opaleye * Level 6: 7 MP Cost Summons such as Norwegian Ridgeback Egg * Level 7: Thestral Card The Creature Card will be upgraded 1 time if you use a 3-4 MP Spell Card, 2 times if you use a 5-6 MP Spell Card, and 3 times if you use a 7 MP Spell Card. However, using a 5-7 MP Spell Card to upgrade your Creature Card more than once when it is already at the 5th level (i.e. offering a 6 MP Summon) will still give you a 6th level Creature Card (7 MP Summon) rather than upgrading straight to the Thestral Card.
