# Gold

> 英文名：Gold


## 简介（英文原文）

Gold is one of the currencies the player can accumulate in game. It is primarily used to upgrade Cards and Echoes, as well as to purchase items from Shops. It can be earned in a variety of ways, most consistently through Into the Woods (Gold) in the Forbidden Forest.
