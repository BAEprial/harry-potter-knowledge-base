## Biography（英文原文）

Kal has performed music since he was very young. His first use of magic was even music-related: while playing a piano at the age of five he used magic to twinkle the keys he couldn’t reach. He’s been enchanting musical instruments ever since. While at Hogwarts he delighted his fellow students by playing impromptu concerts on the shore of the Black Lake, often attracting the attention of the Merpeople who seemed to like his music. After finishing school Kal considered a professional career in the Muggle music business, but a story from his granny changed everything...
