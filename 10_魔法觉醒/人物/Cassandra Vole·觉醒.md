## 简介（英文原文）

Cassandra Vole is a young Witch who started her education at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry during the same year as the Player, the Sorting Hat having sorted her into the Slytherin house during the Sorting Ceremony of 2008.

## Biography（英文原文）

===First Year=== On 1 September 2008, Cassandra boarded the Hogwarts Express and sat in a compartment with Colby and Fischer Frey. When another first-year student asked if they could join them, Cassandra immediately refused and told them to find somewhere else. Upon arriving at Hogwarts, she was sorted into Slytherin House. Early on, she developed a reputation for bullying fellow students, including Ivy Warrington and Daniel Page. After their first Charms lesson, the student saw Cassandra in a corridor practicing the Levitation Charm on a feather. When they complimented her, she confidently said she was naturally talented. When the student admitted they wished they could improve, Cassandra simply told them it was “just a swish and flick.” She then invited them to join the Duelling Club if they weren’t afraid, as Colby and Fischer arrived. Before the duels began, Cassandra warned the student not to make a scene when they lost. Initially, the student duelled Colby and won. ## Physical Description（英文原文）

Cassandra is often said to resemble her great-grandmother Delilah in her younger years. She has dark green eyes, pale skin with red undertones, and long yellow-blonde hair that usually rests over her left shoulder, with a few loose strands framing her face. A green bow typically adorns the left side of her head, and she wears green triangle-shaped earrings accented with gold. Green is unmistakably her signature color, she is never seen without it in her outfit. She also frequently wears white gloves as part of her polished appearance. While she dons the standard Hogwarts school uniform during classes, Cassandra prefers a more distinct style outside of them. Her usual attire includes a black bolero over a white blouse, a green ribbon tie, and a high-waisted green skirt. Among her classmates, Cassandra is considered a pretty girl, mentioned in the same breath as Robyn Thistlethwaite and Ivy Warrington. However, despite her beauty, she is often described as a “nightmare” due to her haughty demeanor and the way she treats those who try to speak with her. ## Personality and Traits（英文原文）

Cassandra has a mean-spirited and conceited personality. She takes pleasure in belittling those she perceives as beneath her, often doing so with her classmates, particularly Ivy Warrington and Daniel Page. Entitled and self-assured, Cassandra always assumes she is right and rarely tolerates dissent. She sees herself as more attractive, more talented, and more popular than any other witch at Hogwarts. Anyone who disagrees with this belief, is, in her eyes, clearly a fool in need of correction. She has a passion for fashion and frequently criticizes other students for their style choices, using it as another avenue to assert her superiority. Cassandra also firmly believes that not all witches and wizards are created equal, and she is eager to make sure others understand their “place.” She dislikes Quidditch and prefers Herbology, a subject she genuinely enjoys and excels at. Despite her posh and arrogant demeanor, Cassandra is not entirely heartless. One Christmas, she is genuinely delighted when Colby Frey gifts her a scarf he made himself.
