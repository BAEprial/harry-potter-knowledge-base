# Lottie Turner·觉醒

> 英文名：Lottie Turner


## 简介（英文原文）

Lottie Turner is a young Witch who started her education at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry during the same year as the Player, the Sorting Hat having sorted her into the same house the Player chooses during the Sorting Ceremony of 2008.
