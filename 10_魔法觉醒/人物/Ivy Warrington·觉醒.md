> 英文名：Ivy Warrington


## 简介（英文原文）

Ivy Warrington is a young Witch who started her education at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry during the same year as the Player, the Sorting Hat having sorted her into the same house the Player chooses during the Sorting Ceremony of 2008.

## Biography（英文原文）

===First Year=== At age eleven, Ivy begins attending Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. On the Hogwarts Express, she notices a familiar student struggling to find a seat. She invites them to sit with her, excitedly sharing how thrilled she is to be going to Hogwarts. They admit they're nervous, but Ivy assures them that the castle is amazing and she can’t wait to explore it. However, during the Sorting Ceremony, Ivy mysteriously goes missing. She is sorted later than the others, joining the same house as the student she met—alongside Daniel Page and Lottie Turner. Ivy explains that she got lost while wandering the castle, looking for something she couldn’t quite remember. She feels like someone is following her, and eventually, the Room of Requirement appears. Inside, she waits until Daniel and the Player find her sitting on a trunk. She’s relieved to be found but insists the trunk isn’t hers, so they leave it behind. Back in the common room, Ivy practices spells with Daniel and the Player. She effortlessly performs a levitation charm, impressing them. ## Relationships（英文原文）

===Family=== Ivy adores her twin sister, Winifred. When their father decides not to send Winifred to Hogwarts, Ivy tries to persuade him with the help of Hogwarts professors. However, Winifred’s resentment eventually overwhelms her, and she vanishes before Ivy’s eyes using a spell of her own. Ivy is devastated. Even after her memories of Winifred are erased, her Boggart still appears as herself, symbolizing the lingering emotional damage. She has a relatively good relationship with her maternal grandmother, whom she associates with the warmth of home and the taste of apple and gooseberry oliebollen made every Christmas. Despite this closeness, Ivy grows increasingly suspicious that her grandmother is hiding something. Eventually, she discovers that her grandmother erased all memory of Winifred to protect her mind. In truth, Winifred never disappeared—she has been unconscious and resting in St. Mungo’s for three years. Ivy is left confused and hurt, questioning why her grandmother never checked the hospital. ## Physical Description（英文原文）

Ivy appears younger than her actual age, with fair skin, long straight silver hair that covers the right side of her face, and striking purple eyes. She always wears a shimmering blue locket around her neck. While she closely resembles her twin sister Winifred, the main difference lies in their eyes, Winifred shares their mother’s blue eyes, while Ivy inherited their grandmother’s violet ones. She typically wears her school uniform robes paired with long brown boots and carries a book-shaped bag slung over her shoulder. Outside of class, she prefers a plum-colored checked dress, a white cardigan, and white stockings. ## Personality and Traits（英文原文）

Ivy is an energetic and confident witch, known for her impressive wandwork and strong desire to prove herself. Before attending Hogwarts, she was already well-versed in advanced spells and charms, having been taught by her maternal grandmother. Smart, passionate, and fiercely loyal to her friends, she never hesitates to stand up for them in the face of conflict. She shares a particularly strong bond with Daniel Page and the Muggle-born student who helped find her when she went missing in the Room of Requirement. Ivy often helps Daniel or enjoys teasing him playfully. She strongly disapproves of prejudice against non-magical people and believes in equality, though she remains deeply distrustful of the group known as NOTME and is firmly against their methods. Despite her outward optimism and bright personality, Ivy sometimes feels like something is missing. She experiences episodes of memory loss and sleepwalking—on her first night at Hogwarts, she wandered through several corridors and staircases in her sleep.
