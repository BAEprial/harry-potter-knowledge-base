> 英文名：Daniel Page


## 简介（英文原文）

Daniel Page is a young Wizard who started his education at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry during the same year as the Player, the Sorting Hat having sorted him into the same house as the Player during the Sorting Ceremony of 2008.

## Biography（英文原文）

===First Year=== Daniel Page sat alone on the Hogwarts Express, isolating himself from the other students. When a fellow first-year asked if they could sit beside him, Daniel coldly replied that they wouldn’t want to, and that no one ever did. Arriving at Hogwarts, Daniel felt a sigh of relief. Away from the scrutiny of the Muggle world and the press, he could finally breathe. Few in the wizarding world read Muggle newspapers, and thankfully, none of his peers seemed to know about his family’s past. At the Sorting Ceremony, Daniel was placed into the same house as Ivy Warrington, Lottie Turner, and another unnamed student. One day, he ran into that same student on the Grand Staircase while they were fleeing from Argus Filch. Daniel told them Filch was also chasing him, accusing him of stealing potion ingredients, which he firmly denied. As they caught their breath, a portrait nearby, Sir William de Cognac, called the scene "delightful drama" and advised them to stay calm. ## Relationships（英文原文）

====Family==== Daniel Page deeply cares for his family and wants nothing more than to bring them back together. Although his relationship with his father and older brother, Reggie, remains largely unclear, he holds a strong emotional attachment to the family as a whole, which often frustrates his older sister, Esme, who wants him to disown them entirely. While Daniel accepts his father and brother’s criminal actions to some extent, he firmly defends his mother, Kaleena. He believes she never intended to harm anyone or violate the International Statute of Wizarding Secrecy. Her imprisonment in Azkaban deeply upsets him, especially since she is convicted of being a member of the Nocturnal Order of Tricks and Magical Exhibitions without proper evidence. To Daniel, her sentence is unfair. He shares a close and loving bond with Kaleena, being the only magically gifted child in the family and learning much about the wizarding world from her. ## Personality and Traits（英文原文）

Upon arriving at school, Daniel comes off as a solitary, studious young wizard who prefers focusing on academics over socializing. He strives to be the best in all magical subjects and is determined to prove himself. Rather than making friends, he favors mixing potions and spending time alone in quiet places like the Potions Classroom or the Forbidden Forest. When he eventually befriends Robyn Thistlethwaite, Ivy Warrington, and others, Daniel begins to open up and becomes noticeably more sociable. Daniel is a blunt individual, never afraid to say what’s on his mind, and he’s known for his dry, sarcastic wit. He talks back to adults and isn’t always tactful with his friends, sometimes coming off as insensitive or rude. He can be socially awkward and dislikes attending social events. By his own admission, he’s bad at picking gifts, uninterested in fashion, and uncomfortable with attention, especially the kind that stems from his family's unfortunate reputation.
