## Biography（英文原文）

===Academic Story=== Being a Muggle-born, the Player doesn't know about the wizarding world during their childhood but did notice that they could make strange things happen. Their abilities drew stares and whispers which they grew used to. While wishing there was a place they could belong, Rubeus Hagrid arrived at their house with their acceptance letter to attend Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. The next day, they visited Diagon Alley to get school supplies. While at the wall outside the Leaky Cauldron, the Player has to tap on three bricks with Hagrid's umbrella to open up the way to Diagon Alley. They shop in Madam Malkin's Robe Shop where the Player customizes their character. After that, they get a wand at Ollivander's Wand Shop. Garrick Ollivander thought there was something familiar about them while giving them a wand to try. The first wand does not agree with them, so they try out a second wand. When it does agree with them, Ollivander finds it very curious. The wands are random which means it is different for every Player.
