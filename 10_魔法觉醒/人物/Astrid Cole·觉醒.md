## Physical Description（英文原文）

Much like a living Devil's Snare, Cole is a tall, lanky individual with dark and gothic outfits, irresistibly drawn to dark and dangerous plants.

## Personality and Traits（英文原文）

Cole's intimidating exterior conceals a brilliant and benevolent force for good, adept at harnessing the healing power of even the darkest plants. The reason that she’s attracted to Herbology because she sees the power it has to help people—a principle instilled by her mum (a Ministry worker) and her dad (a policeman).
