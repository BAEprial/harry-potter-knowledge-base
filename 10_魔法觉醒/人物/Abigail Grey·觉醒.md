# Abigail Grey·觉醒

> 英文名：Abigail Grey


## 简介（英文原文）

Abigail is a young Witch afflicted with lycanthropy who attended Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry at the same time as the Player. She was sorted into Gryffindor during a Sorting Ceremony. She appears in the fourth Season of Harry Potter: Magic Awakened entitled Where's Wolf? released in December 2023.

## Biography（英文原文）

Abigail first appears in Hogsmeade where she confronts Renka, who is anti-werewolf. The village is experiencing strange occurrences including a strange mist, break-ins and the lights going on and off. Renka is blaming it on the "mindless beasts" that are werewolves. Abigail points out it cannot be werewolves and that they are people, not beasts. Renka argues with her. The Player intervenes to support Abigail but Renka writes them off as rude and naive. Abigail runs away in tears with Player running after her. They comfort her but Abigail is angry and claims werewolves are misunderstood. Later on, the player is in the Library when they realise Abigail is stalking them. She says it is because nobody has ever supported her before. She tells the Player Professor McGonagall is considering stopping Hogsmeade visits unless it is safer to go there. They agree to go back to Hogsmeade to investigate the strange occurrences to find the real culprit. Abigail says she has to be back to Hogwarts Castle by dark.
