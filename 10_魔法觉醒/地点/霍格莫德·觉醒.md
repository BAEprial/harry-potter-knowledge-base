# 霍格莫德·觉醒

> 英文名：Hogsmeade


## 简介（英文原文）

Hogsmeade is a location that opened up on 12 December 2023 (Along with Season 4 : Where Wolves?) Zonko's Joke Shop and the Three Broomsticks Inn have previously appeared in the Main Story.

## Locations（英文原文）

* Hogsmeade Post Office (opened on 2 April 2024) * Zonko's Joke Shop (opened on 26 December 2023)(1 April 2023 for CN server) * Honeydukes Sweetshop (opened on 12 November 2023) * Three Boomsticks Inn (opened on 12 November 2023) * Madam Puddifoot's Tea Shop (opens on 23 December 2023) * Gladrags Wizardwear (opened on 8 January 2024) * Shrieking Shack (opened on 12 December 2023) * Hog's Head Inn (opened on 12 December 2023) * Bewildering Blooms (opened on 6 February 2024) * Leave Confiserie
