# Bewildering Blooms

> 英文名：Bewildering Blooms


## 简介（英文原文）

Bewildering Blooms was a magical plant shop located in the all-wizarding village Hogsmeade and owned by Yubert Thorne during the 2010s. It sold a variety of magical plants, including Dirigible Plums, Fanged Geraniums, and Puffapods.

## Bewildering Blooms（英文原文）

This shop opened in the season "The Mysterious Malady" in 6 February to 19 March 2024. Bewildering Blooms was a magical plant shop located in the all-wizarding village Hogsmeade and owned by Yubert Thorne during the 2010s.
