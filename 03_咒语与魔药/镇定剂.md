# 镇定剂

> 英文名：Calming Draught

## 条目内容（英文原文）

- **效果**：Calms the drinker
- **配料**：Lavender, Crocodile heart, Peppermint
- **难度**：Moderate to Advanced
- **特征**：Blue or brown in colour
- **副作用**：Unpleasant results if consumed by certain animals, Burning mouth sensation, watery eyes or uncontrollable sobbing if too much peppermint oil added
