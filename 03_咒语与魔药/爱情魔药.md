# 爱情魔药

> 英文名：Love potion

## 条目内容（英文原文）

- **效果**：Infatuation with whoever offered the potion
- **配料**：Ashwinder eggs, Rose thorns, Peppermint, Powdered Moonstone, Pearl Dust, Rose Petals
- **难度**：Advanced
- **特征**：Pink in colour
- **副作用**：Embarrassment on the part of the drinker
- **发明者**：Laverne de Montmorency, Fred Weasley, George Weasley
